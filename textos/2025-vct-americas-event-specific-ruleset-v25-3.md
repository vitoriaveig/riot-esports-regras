1.   Introduction and Purpose
      1.1.   Background
      VALORANT Champions Tour LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (“League Entity”),
      along with its affiliates (collectively, “Riot”), organizes a series of regional, cross-regional,
      and international esports tournaments and related media events under the name
      VALORANT Champions Tour (the “VCT”) which feature competitive play (both live and
      online) of the video game VALORANT (the “Game”) by professional esports players and
      teams. The VCT is composed of three member leagues: (i) VCT Americas, (ii) VCT Pacific,
      and (iii) VCT EMEA (each such league, a “VCT League” or “League”). The League Entity
      has entered into written agreements with certain team-owning entities (each, a “Team
      Entity”) governing right to compete in the VCT and their respective Leagues (each such
      agreement, a “Team Participation Agreement”). Pursuant to the Team Participation
      Agreements and as more fully described therein, the League Entity has granted each
      Team Entity the right to field a team that will compete in the VCT and its respective
      League (“Team”).
      1.2.   Purpose
      The League Entity has created this VCT Americas Event-Specific Ruleset (the
      “Event-Specific Ruleset”) to set forth certain policies, rules, and procedures that will
      apply to all Teams participating in the VCT Americas event, as well as their professional
      players signed to a Team’s official roster (each, a “Player”), owners, Coaches, managers
      (collectively with Players, “Team Members”), and other employees. This Event-Specific
      Ruleset is in addition to, and not in lieu of, the VALORANT Official Competition Ruleset
      (the “Competition Ruleset”) and the VCT25 Roster Construction Rules (the “RCR”).

2.   VCT Americas Event Structure
      2.1.   Kickoff Tournament Format
      Twelve teams will play in a Double-Elimination bracket, with teams being seeded based
      on 2024 VCT performance. The four (4) teams that participated in 2024 VCT Champions
      will be placed in one group, while the remaining eight (8) teams will be placed in
      another. Teams from the first group will be granted first-round byes and randomly drawn
      into second-round Matches. Teams from the second group will be randomly drawn into
      first-round Matches. Each Match will be best-of-three (“Bo3”), with the exception of the
      Lower Final and Grand Final, which will be best-of-five (“Bo5”).
      
      For each Match, Team A and Team B in the Map Selection Process (as defined in Section
      4.6.8 of the Competition Ruleset) will be determined either by seeding or coin-toss. For
      Upper Bracket Round 2 matches, the team that was granted a first-round Bye will be
      considered the higher seeded team. For Lower Bracket Matches, the team that fell into
      the Lower Bracket later will be considered the higher seeded team. For Matches where
      control of the Map Selection Process is determined by coin toss, Team A and B will be
      directly determined by the result of the toss. For clarity, Teams will not choose to be
      Team A or B in the Map Selection Process. The two sides of the coin will represent the
      two possibilities of Team A or B in a given Match.
              2.1.1. Upper Bracket Round 1
                     Upper Bracket Round 1 will consist of four best-of-three Matches,
                     designated M1 through M4. The winner of each Upper Bracket Round 1
                     Match shall advance to play the teams granted first-round Byes in Upper
                     Bracket Round 2. The losers shall drop down to Lower Bracket Round 1.
              2.1.2. Upper Bracket Round 2
                     Upper Bracket Round 2 will consist of four best-of-three Matches,
                     designated M5 through M8. The winner of each Upper Bracket Round 2
                     Match shall advance to play each other in the Upper Bracket Semi-Finals.
                     The losing teams shall drop down to Lower Bracket Round 1.
              2.1.3. Lower Bracket Round 1
                      Lower Bracket Round 1 will consist of four best-of-three Matches,
                      designated M9 through M12. The winner of each Lower Bracket Round 1
                      Match shall advance to play each other in Lower Bracket Round 2. The
                      losing teams shall be eliminated from the tournament.
              2.1.4. Upper Bracket Semifinals
                     Upper Bracket Semi-Finals will consist of two best-of-three Matches,
                     designated M13 and M14. The winner of each Match shall advance to
                     the Upper Bracket Final. The losing teams shall drop into Lower Bracket
                     Semi-Finals.

performance. The first and second place teams will be placed into a pool and drawn into
Group A or Group B. The third and fourth place teams will be placed into a pool and
drawn into Group A or Group B. Both fifth place teams will be placed into a pool and

drawn into Group A or Group B. Finally, all four ninth place teams will be placed into a
pool and drawn into Group A or Group B. Teams within each group will play each other
in best-of-three Round Robin matches. Each Group Stage match win will be worth 1
Championship Point toward 2025 VCT Champions.

4.6.8 of the Competition Ruleset) will be determined by coin toss.

2.6.   Stage 2 Format - Group Stage
Following the conclusion of Stage 1 Playoffs, the two groups from Stage 1 will have their
teams shuffled via group draw. Teams will be divided into three groups of two pairs,
based on the final Stage 1 Regular Season standings. The first place teams will be paired
together, followed by the second place teams, and so on through the sixth place teams.
The groups will consist of the first and second place team pairs, the third and fourth
place team pairs, and the fifth and sixth place team pairs, respectively. One pairing will
be randomly drawn from each group, and that pair of teams will switch between Groups
Alpha and Omega, ultimately resulting in half the teams from each Stage 1 group
switching.

After the groups are finalized, teams within each group will play each other in
best-of-three Round Robin matches. Each Group Stage match win will be worth 1
Championship Point toward the 2025 VCT Champions.

3.   Roster Rules
      3.1.   Starting Rosters
      For each match of the VCT Americas Competition, the Team must designate ﬁve (5)
      starters from the Team’s Active Roster (as defined in the RCR), which shall constitute the
      Team’s “Starting Roster”. Rosters are considered public at the submission deadline.
      Requests to modify a Starting Roster for a Team’s first match on any day must be
      submitted no later than 8:00pm local time, or an hour after the conclusion of the final
      game of the day, whichever is the latest, the day prior to the team’s match.
      3.2.   VCT Transfer Window
      The VCT 2025 transfer window closes on August 14, 2025 at 5:00pm in the time zone of
      a League’s Hub City (“VCT Transfer Window”) (see Section 1.2. of VCT25 Roster
      Construction Rules).
      Once the Transfer Window closes, Teams are prohibited from adding any player to their
      Full Team Roster (as defined in the VCT25 Roster Construction Rules).
      3.3.   Substitutions
      For Matches involving more than one map (i.e., a best-of-three or best-of-five Match), a
      Team may replace their then-current starters with substitutes from the Team’s Active
      Roster (as defined in the VCT25 Roster Construction Rules) in between maps, provided
      that the Team informs the opposing Team and receives approval from the referees of
      such substitution no later than two (2) minutes after the conclusion of the previous

4.   Equipment
      4.1.   Team-Owned Equipment
      For all LAN Matches as a part of VCT Americas, Players are allowed to bring the following
      categories of equipment, which are owned by themselves or their Teams, into the Match
      Area (as defined in Section 3.3 of the Competition Ruleset) and can use such equipment
      during the LAN event: (1) PC keyboards, (2) PC mice and cord holders, and (3)
      mousepads (collectively, “Team-Owned Equipment”). For the avoidance of doubt,
      players may not bring, use or wear any in-ear headphones, headsets, and/or
      microphones that are not provided by League Officials.
      4.2.   League-Provided Equipment
      The League Entity will provide, and players will exclusively use, equipment in the
      following categories for all VCT and League LAN events: (1) PC and monitor, (2) hand
      warmers, (3) headsets and microphones, (4) in-ear headphones, and (5) tables and
      chairs. At the request of a player, the League Entity will provide the following categories
      of equipment for use in all VCT and League LAN events: (1) PC keyboards, (2) PC mice,
      and (3) mouse pads. All equipment provided by the League Entity (collectively,
      “League-Provided Equipment”) will be chosen, selected and determined at the sole
      discretion of the League Entity. Subject to the foregoing, the unauthorized modification
      of League-Provided Equipment or the use of hardware, software or other equipment
      that is not provided or approved by League Officials will be deemed cheating. Players
      must return all League-Provided Equipment to League Officials when the LAN event is
      over or upon request by League Officials. Team Members may be penalized for
      destruction of League-Provided Equipment.

4.3.   Peripheral Policy
All Team-Owned Equipment must be submitted to League Officials in advance for
approval. Approved equipment will remain on-site with League Officials and will only be
accessible before the Match or at such time as approved by the Head Referee.
Unapproved equipment or equipment that is suspected by League Officials of providing
an unfair competitive advantage will not be permitted for use, and Players will be
required to use League-Provided Equipment instead. At their discretion, League Officials
may disallow the use of any individual piece of equipment for reasons relating to
tournament security, safety or operational efficiency or effectiveness. No Player-owned
or Team-owned hardware or equipment may be brought into the Match Area if it
features or displays any name, likeness or logo of a company or brand which is a
competitor of Riot Games or VALORANT. If a team wishes to make changes to their
team-owned equipment, notice must be provided to League Officials at least one (1)
week prior to the team’s next match.
4.4.   Native Programs
Players and Coaches may use the native programs MS Paint and Notepad before and
after Matches. Players may not use the native programs MS Paint and Notepad during a
Match, and must close out of those programs before the start of the Match. Coaches
may use the native programs MS Paint and Notepad during a Match.
League Officials may prohibit Players and Coaches from accessing native programs in
cases of abuse.
4.5.   Audio Restrictions
Players and Coaches will be required to maintain volume levels above minimum settings.
League Officials may require Players and Coaches to adjust their volume levels higher if
the officials determine, at their sole discretion, that volume levels are too low.
Headphones must be placed directly on a Player or Coach’s ears, and must remain there
for the duration of the Match. Players and Coaches are not permitted to obstruct the
placement of headphones by any method or place any item, scarves or other articles of
clothing, between the headphones and the Player and Coach’s ears, except for certain
religious articles and/or medical devices (e.g., hijab, dastar, yarmulke and hearing
devices). The determination of whether an item qualifies for the religious and/or
medical exemption will be made by League Officials at their sole discretion.

5.   Venue, Competition Area Layout, and Schedule
      5.1.   Match Area
      The “Match Area” is the area immediately surrounding any competition PCs used during
      Match play at a LAN or online event. During Match play, the presence of Team Members
      in the Match Area is restricted solely to the Players of the Teams in play that are
      participating in the Match, and the Match Coaches in accordance with Section 5.1.1.
              5.1.1. Coach Match Area Access
                      The Team may designate a minimum of one (1) and a maximum of two
                      (2) coaches that will participate on stage during the Match (“Match
                      Coaches”), one of which is required to be the Head Coach. The Match
                      Coaches for Teams participating in a given day’s Matches will be granted
                      Match Area access during those Matches, and will be allowed to
                      communicate with their Team during the map pick/ban, agent selection
                      process, and during Timeouts. Match Coaches must be declared at 5pm
                      local time two calendar days before the start of a Competitive Week for
                      that Week. Teams may remove, but not add, Match Coaches during or
                      between Matches in any given Competitive Week. Teams must require
                      valid proof of work eligibility for any elected Match Coach.

6.   Match Process
      6.1.   Player Ready State
      No fewer than two minutes before an official Match is scheduled to begin, a referee will
      confirm with each Player that their setup is complete and will instruct all Players to enter
      the Match lobby. Once all ten Players in a Match have confirmed completion of setup
      and have entered the Match lobby, Players may not enter a warm-up Match or leave the
      Match Area. Once all ten Players in a Match have confirmed completion of setup and
      have entered the Match lobby, all settings will be considered complete and no pauses or
      delay of the Match will be allowed for minor settings issues.

7.   Competitive Patch
      Matches are scheduled to be played on the patches provided by the League. The
      competitive patch schedule is subject to change and the League will notify teams upon
      any modification.

8.   Map Pool
      The map pool for Kickoff consists of Abyss, Bind, Fracture, Haven, Lotus, Pearl, and Split.
      Pursuant to the VALORANT Champions Tour VALORANT Official Competition Ruleset
