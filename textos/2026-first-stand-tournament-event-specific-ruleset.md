1. Background and Purpose
   1.1. Purpose.
             League of Legends Championship Series LLC, a Delaware limited liability
             company (“LE Entity”), along with its affiliates (collectively, “Riot”) has created
             this 2026 First Stand Tournament Event-Specific Ruleset (the “Event-Specific
             Ruleset”) to set forth certain policies, rules, and procedures that will apply to all
             Teams participating in the First Stand Tournament event. This Event-Specific
             Ruleset is in addition to, and not in lieu of, the League of Legends Esports
             International Events 2026 Competition Ruleset (the “Competition Ruleset”)
             and the League of Legends Esports: Global Policies (the “Global Policies”).

2. Event Eligibility
   2.1.     Team Member Eligibility
             To be eligible to participate in the First Stand Tournament (“FST”), all players,
             coaches, and managers (“Team Members”) must satisfy the following
             conditions:

           2.1.1.   Age. Each Team Member must have been born on or before March 16,
                     2008 (i.e. at least 17 years old by the first day of the event).

           2.1.2.   Residency and Work Eligibility. Each Team Member must meet the work
                     eligibility requirements for the region they are representing at the FST.

           2.1.3.   No Riot Employees. Team Members may not be employees of Riot
                     Games Inc. (“RGI”) or League of Legends Esports Federation LLC or any
                     of their respective affiliates at the start of or at any point during the FST.
                     “Affiliate” is defined as any person or other entity which owns or controls,
                     is under the ownership or control of, or is under common ownership or
                     control with, an Owner. “Control” shall mean the power, through any
                     means, to determine the policies or management of an entity, whether
                     through the power to elect, appoint or approve, directly or indirectly, the
                     directors, officers, managers or trustees of such entity or otherwise.

           2.1.4.   Team Member Handles. A Team Member must use the tournament
                     handle the Team Member last used in regional play whilst qualifying for
                     FST. Name changes are not allowed except under certain extenuating
                     circumstances, and must be approved by FST officials prior to use in a
                     FST game.

2.2.     Team Eligibility
        2.2.1.   Team Qualification. Teams will qualify for the FST through regular and
                  postseason play from the region in which they normally compete, and by
                  the rules set forth by that region. Regions have been awarded the
                  following number of berths:

              2.2.1.1.   China (LPL), South Korea (LCK): 2 Berths

              2.2.1.2.   Asia Pacific (LCP), Brazil (CBLOL), EMEA (LEC), North America
                          (LCS): 1 Berth

                  Throughout this ruleset, teams may be referred to by their region and a
                  number, i.e. LCK2 or LCS1. This number refers to the ranking which they
                  achieved through regional play.

        2.2.2.   Qualification Revocation. Teams may have their qualification revoked in
                  any of the following situations:

              2.2.2.1.   The qualified team is not able to travel to the event location due to
                          local / global travel restrictions, or fail to obtain necessary
                          documents for entering and remaining in any event location
                          country for the duration of the competition (such as visas, travel
                          clearance, etc.) before traveling to FST.

              2.2.2.2.   The qualified team is unable to fulfill the roster requirements by the
                          deadline specified by FST Officials.

              2.2.2.3.   Other situations that FST Officials deemed to be necessary to
                          prohibit a qualified team from participating in FST.

        2.2.3.   Team Names, Tags, and Logos. Each team must use the Team Name,
                  Team Tag, and Team Logo the team last used in regional play whilst
                  qualifying for FST. Changes are not allowed except under certain
                  extenuating circumstances, and must be approved by FST officials prior to
                  use.

3. Rosters
   3.1.     Minimum Roster Requirements
           3.1.1.   Players. Each team must maintain, at all times during the FST, a roster of
                     at least five Players. A minimum of three of these players must be
                     considered Residents of the region they are representing. Teams may
                     additionally maintain one or two more Players (for a total of six or seven)
                     on their roster.

           3.1.2.   Coaches. Each team must maintain, at all times during the FST, one Head
                     Coach. Teams may additionally maintain up to three Strategic Coaches on
                     their roster.

           3.1.3.   Manager. Each team must maintain, at all times during the FST, one
                     Team Manager.

   3.2.     Roster Deadlines
           3.2.1.   Active Roster Lock. Teams must finalize and submit their Active Roster to
                     FST officials no later than February 20th, 2026 at 17:59 PST. The Active
                     Roster submitted at this time will be considered the eligible Active Roster
                     for the FST. This roster may only be composed of players and coaches
                     that are on a team’s regional roster. Any changes to a team’s Active Roster
                     after the Roster Lock date will be governed by the Roster Emergency
                     Policy in the LoL Esports Global Policies.

           3.2.2.   Starting Rosters. For each game at the FST, teams must designate five
                     players from their Active Roster as that game’s Starting Roster. Roster
                     submissions for a Team’s first game on any day must be submitted no
                     later than 5:00 pm local time, or an hour after the conclusion of the final
                     game of the day, whichever is the latest, the day prior to the team’s
                     match. Roster submissions for subsequent games must be submitted no
                     later than 5 minutes after the conclusion of the previous game.

   3.3.     Attendance.
             All Team Members must be present for all required activities, including, but not
             limited to:

           3.3.1.   Scheduled Matches

4.   Tournament Structure, Schedule, and Prizing
      4.1.     Competitive Format
              4.1.1.   Group Stage.

                    4.1.1.1.   Description. Eight teams will be drawn into two best-of-five,
                                double elimination brackets. The top two teams coming out of
                                each bracket (the 2-0 and 2-1 team) will take their place in the
                                Bracket Stage. A visual representation of the Group Stage is
                                available in the Appendix as Figure 1.

                          4.1.1.1.1.   Each bracket will consist of five best-of-five matches - two
                                        Round 1 matches, two Round 2 matches, and one Round 3
                                        match.

                          4.1.1.1.2.   The winners of the Round 1 matches will advance to Round
                                        2, Match 1, while the losers will advance to the Round 2,
                                        Match 2.

                          4.1.1.1.3.   The winners of Round 2, Match 1 will advance to the
                                        Bracket Stage as the 1st Seed from their group. The losers
                                        of Round 2, Match 2 will be eliminated from the FST as the
                                        7th/8th place finishers.

                          4.1.1.1.4.   The losers of Round 2 - Match 1 will face the winners of
                                        Round 2 - Match 2 in Round 3. The losers of these matches
                                        will be eliminated from the FST as the 5th/6th place
                                        finishers, while the winners will move on to the Bracket
                                        Stage as the 2nd Seed from their group.

              4.1.2.   Knockout Stage

                    4.1.2.1.   Description. Four teams will be drawn into a best-of-five, single
                                elimination bracket with two rounds: Semifinals and Finals. A visual
                                representation of the Knockout Stage is available in the Appendix
                                as Figure 2.

                          4.1.2.1.1.   The 1st Seed of Group A will play the 2nd Seed of Group B
                                        and vice versa.

<!-- página 8 -->
            4.1.2.1.2.   Teams that win their matches in the semifinals will advance
                          to the finals. Teams that lose their matches will be
                          eliminated from the FST as 3rd/4th place finishers.

            4.1.2.1.3.   The team that wins the finals will be the 2026 FST
                          Champion. The team that loses the finals will be eliminated
                          from the FST as the 2nd place finisher.

4.1.3.   First Selection.

      4.1.3.1.   For every match at FST one team will earn the Right of First
                  Selection (“RoFS”). The team with RoFS may select between two
                  pre-game choices: their preferred pick position or their preferred
                  map side. Their opponent will be granted their preference on the
                  choice that the initial team did not select.

            4.1.3.1.1.   As an example, if the team with RoFS selected “First Pick,”
                          the opponent would be able to select “Blue Side” or “Red
                          Side.”

      4.1.3.2.   Round Specifics:

            4.1.3.2.1.   For Round 1 of the FST Group Stage, RoFS will be granted
                          to the team with a higher Draw Pool, or if Draw Pool is
                          equal, it will be determined by draw order or other
                          equivalent method.

            4.1.3.2.2.   For Rounds 2 and 3 of the FST Group Stage, RoFS will be
                          determined by coinflip or other equivalent method.

            4.1.3.2.3.   For the Semifinals, RoFS will be granted to the team with a
                          better Group Stage match record.

            4.1.3.2.4.   For the Finals, RoFS will be determined by coinflip or other
                          equivalent method.

      4.1.3.3.   Submission Timing.

            4.1.3.3.1.   Each Team will make their selections simultaneously and
                          will submit them blindly to FST Officials. To enable this
                          process, the team with RoFS must submit their
                          selection/choice (i.e., “Red Side”), and the team without
                          RoFS must submit their selections for both choices (i.e.
                          “First Pick, Red Side”). The selection that is not used will be

                                 For pre-determined RoFS (i.e. when decided by Draw Pool),
                                 the Selection Submissions are due at 17:59 PM the night
                                 before the match, or one hour following the completion of
                                 matches if a team is competing on the day the submission
                                 is due. For randomly determined RoFS for Group Stage
                                 Round 1, the Selection Submissions are due 24 hours after
                                 the completion of the FST Draw Show. For randomly
                                 determined RoFS outside of Group Stage Round 1, the

              4.1.3.4.   Subsequent Games. Within any match, for all games after the
                          first, the losing Team of the previous game will have the RoFS.
                          Both Selection Submissions will be due within 5 minutes of the end
                          of the previous game.

4.2.     Tournament Seeding
          Teams have been seeded into the tournament to start in one of three draw pools:

        4.2.1.   Pool 1: LCK1, LPL1

        4.2.2.   Pool 2: CBLOL1, LCP1, LCS1, LEC1

        4.2.3.   Pool 3: LCK2, LPL2



4.3.     Tournament Draws
        4.3.1.   Group Stage Draw

              4.3.1.1.   Draw Procedure. Teams will be drawn into matches by random
                          selection against an opponent from their matched pool (i.e., Pool 1
                          vs Pool 3, and Pool 2 vs Pool 2). The order of the draw will not
                          determine the order in which the matches are played.

              4.3.1.2.   Draw Restrictions. Each Group will only be allowed a single team
                          per region

6.   Spirit of the Rules
      6.1.   Finality of Decisions
              All decisions regarding the interpretation of these Rules, Player eligibility,
              scheduling and staging of the FST, and penalties for misconduct, lie solely with
              the FST, the decisions of which are final. FST decisions with respect to these
              Rules cannot be appealed and shall not give rise to any claim for monetary
              damages or any other legal or equitable remedy.

      6.2.   Rule Changes
              These Rules may be amended, modified or supplemented by the League, from
              time to time, in order to ensure fair play and the integrity of the League.

      6.3.   Language Differences
              These Rules may be presented in a variety of languages to the competitors of the
              event. In any scenario in which the translation of these rules creates a conflict, the
              English version of this ruleset will prevail.

      6.4.   Best Interests of the FST
              FST Officials at all times may act with the necessary authority to preserve the
              best interests of the FST. This power is not constrained by the lack of any specific
              language in this document. FST Officials may use any form of punitive actions at
              their disposal against any entity whose conduct is not within the confines of the
              best interests of the FST.

Glossary of Terms

Game. An instance of competition on the Summoner’s Rift map that is played until a winner is
determined by one of the following methods, whichever occurs first: (a) completion of the final
objective (destruction of a nexus), (b) Team Surrender, (c) Team Forfeit, or (d) Awarded Game
Victory (see Section 8.8).



Match. A set of games that is played until one team wins a majority of the total games (e.g.,
winning the game in a single game match (“Best of One”); winning two games out of three (“Best
of Three”); winning three games out of five (“Best of Five”)).



Player. An individual athlete who has a written contract for player services with the team they are
playing for, and who is listed as a player for the team on the League of Legends Esports
League-Recognized Contract Database.



Head Coach. An individual who has a written contract for coaching services with the team they
are playing for, and who is listed as a coach for the team on the League of Legends Esports
League-Recognized Contract Database. This individual may not be a player, owner, or manager of
any professional, semi-professional, or academy level team.



Strategic Coach. An individual who has a written contract with the team they are representing.
This individual may be a substitute player, analyst, translator, or other member of the qualified
team. This individual may not act as a head coach without written permission from FST officials.



Manager. The manager is an individual, designated by the team, who is responsible for
administering Team commitments associated with participation in the FST, including, but not
limited to, content requests, Event Schedule notifications, on-site coordination, and information
passthrough. This person may not be a player or the Head Coach for any team, including the one
they are managing.

Team Personnel. Any credentialed member of team staff, including players, coaches, managers,
and other staff.



Active Roster. The six or seven Players and Head Coach representing a professional team.



Qualifying Leagues. The leagues that qualify for the World Championship are the League of
Legends Pro League (LPL), the League of Legends Champions Korea (LCK), the League of
Legends EMEA Championship (LEC), the League of Legends Championship Series of The
Americas (LTA), and the League of Legends Championship Pacific (LCP)
