The ERL Player Database, often mentioned alongside the Global Contract
Database (GCD), is a key tool used in the League of Legends EMEA
region to manage information on players within the European Regional
Leagues (ERLs). Unlike the GCD, which includes players from the LEC and
accredited ERLs, the ERL Player Database specifically tracks players in
non-accredited ERL divisions. It contains essential data such as
registration details, player contracts, and eligibility statuses, helping
maintain compliance and track player movements across the ERL
ecosystem.
