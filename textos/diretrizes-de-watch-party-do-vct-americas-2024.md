COSTREAMER OBLIGATIONS 1/3
Costreamers must accept and abide by certain costreamer obligations set forth
in these terms and conditions, including:


 ●    Complying with Riot’s Legal Jibber Jabber and the terms of service of any
      platform they host a Watch Party on
 ●    Disclosing their material connection with Riot for the tactic
 ●    Moderating the chat on their own Watch Party
 ●    Ensuring that any and all guests follow the rules in the costreaming terms
      and conditions (like those outlined in this presentation)

<!-- página 4 -->
COSTREAMER OBLIGATIONS 2/3
The co-streamer terms and conditions also come with several restrictions.
Among other things, costreamers cannot...

● Block any advertisements from the official stream
● Close/Execute sponsorships deals for the co-stream without authorization
  from Riot Games
● Run personal video ads (Ads run by the streaming platform (youtube or
  twitch) are allowed, but only in the break period between games)
● Use vulgar, threatening or disparaging language
● Promote hatred or discriminatory conduct
● Feature guests or talent under 18 years of age (or minors according to the
  law of the streamer’s country of residence)

<!-- página 5 -->
COSTREAMER OBLIGATIONS 3/3

The co-streamer terms and conditions also come with several restrictions.
Among other things, costreamers cannot...

 ●    Defame or disparage Riot Games, its products, employees, investors,
      sponsors, or players competing in the event

 ●    Run the Watch Party in any way that would violate any applicable laws
      (including anything that might be a criminal offence or give rise to civil
      liability)
