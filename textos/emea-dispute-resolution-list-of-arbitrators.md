                                   LIST OF ARBITRATORS

Filip Blagojević (SRB)

Attorney at law at Bojanovic & Partners in Belgrade, Serbia.

Filip studied law at the University of Belgrade, Serbia. He is admitted to the Belgrade bar association.

Filip is an arbitrator at the European Handball Court of Arbitration and a former member of the Dispute
Resolution Chamber and an arbitrator of the Arbitration Court of the Football Association of Serbia.

https://www.linkedin.com/in/fblaw/



Stephen G. Bock (USA)

Head of Legal and General Counsel of the Fédération Internationale de Volleyball (FIVB) in Lausanne,
Switzerland. As part of his duties with the FIVB, he is responsible for overseeing the financial dispute system
in volleyball.

Stephen studied law at the St. John’s University School of Law in New York, USA. He is admitted to the New
York bar association.

Stephen is an independent administrative review judge of the Union Cycliste Internationale (UCI) (FIVB) and is
a member of the Independent Member Federation Sanctioning Panel of the International Weightlifting
Federation (IWF).

https://www.linkedin.com/in/stephen-bock-02a46225/



Jaime Cambreleng Contreras (ESP)

Attorney at law at Cavaliero & Cambreleng in Geneva, Switzerland.

Jaime studied law at the University Pontificia Comillas (ICADE) in Madrid, Spain. He is admitted to the Madrid
and Geneva bar associations.

Jaime worked at the Fédération Internationale de Football Association (FIFA). He was the head of FIFA’s
litigation department after having been the head of the FIFA disciplinary department.

He is also the deputy chair of the Disciplinary Panel of the Fédération Internationale de Volleyball (FIVB) and
acts as clerk of the FEI Tribunal of the Fédération Equestre Internationale (FEI).

https://www.cavaliero-associates.com/team/jaime-cambreleng-contreras

Catherine Pitre (CAN)

Senior Counsel at World Athletics in Monaco.
