                Section A: Global Policies Introduction
Definitions

  1. Capitalized terms in these LoL Esports Global Policies (these, “Global Policies”), unless
      expressly defined otherwise herein, shall have the meaning ascribed to such terms in
      the glossary (Appendix A).

Official Competitions

                                  Regions of LoL Esports
  2. Riot organizes and sanctions regional, cross-regional, and international esports
      competitions and related media events which feature competitive play (both live and
      online) of the video game League of Legends (LoL).
  3. League of Legends Esports (LoL Esports) features regional esports competitions across
      five major Regions: (i) Americas, (ii) APAC, (iii)China, (iv) EMEA, and (v) South Korea.
                              Riot-sanctioned Competitions
  4. The Professional Leagues of LoL Esports are:
         ● League of the Americas (LTA);
         ● League of Legends Championship Pacific (LCP);
         ● League of Legends Pro League (LPL);
         ● League of Legends EMEA Championship (LEC); and
         ● League of Legends Champions Korea (LCK).
  5. Riot organizes a series of International Events where the best teams from the
      Professional Leagues compete in international esports competitions. These International
      Events are:
         ● First Stand Tournament (FST);
         ● Mid-Season Invitational (MSI); and
         ● League of Legends World Championship (WCE).
  6. Additionally, Riot and other entities sanctioned by Riot organize competitions outside of
      these Professional Leagues and International Events, which include Semi-Professional
      Leagues, developmental leagues, tournaments, cups, and other events.

The Rules

                             Global and Regional Regulations
  7. Riot governs official competitions through regulations, handbooks, and guidance
      documents that may be enacted, updated, and amended from time to time.

  8. All official competitions of LoL Esports are governed by a set of global regulations,
      which include:
         ● The Riot Games Esports Global Code of Conduct, and
         ● These LoL Esports Global Policies.
  9. In addition to these global regulations, each League or event may establish rules that
      are specific to that League or event. These League or event specific rules may add
      further restrictions or limitations to the provisions in these Global Policies.
  10.These regulations and policies collectively form the rules that define and govern each
      respective League, tournament, event, or other official competitions, and the Teams and
      Esports Professionals participating in the aforementioned events.
  11.In the event of a conflict between these Global Policies and other League or event
      specific rules, the provision that is the most protective of the Riot esports ecosystem
      shall govern and prevail.
                                    Guidance Documents
  12.Riot, the League, or the entity organizing a relevant competition may issue
      interpretations, guidance, and other policy or procedural documents from time to time
      in order to explain or supplement these rules. Upon release to teams, all such
      documents shall be deemed to be incorporated into the relevant rules.
                                        Binding Effect
  13.All Esports Professionals accept and agree to be bound by the relevant rules by: (a)
      signing a team participation agreement with a League or other entity organizing an
      official competition of LoL Esports, (b) clicking “Accept” when given the option to do so
      in an online registration process to compete in an official competition, or (c)
      participating in any Riot sanctioned match of a League or other official competition.

Disciplinary Code

                                      Code of Conduct
  14.Riot shall be entitled to undertake disciplinary procedures in relation to any breach of, or
      failure to comply with, any of the aforementioned rules. Breaches of any of these rules
      shall be dealt with in accordance with these rules and the Riot Games Esports Global
      Code of Conduct.
                                       Penalty Indexes
  15.Riot provides an index of breaches and associated penalties designed to promote
      consistency of penalty levels on selected rule violations across LoL Esports (the “Global
      Penalty Index”).
  16.Leagues may provide an additional penalty index that caters to the specific issues and
      situations relevant to their local ecosystem (the “Local Penalty Index”).

                        Section B: Team Operations
Team Ownership Policies

                                 Concurrent Ownership
  18.No Individual or Organization may:
         a. have an Ownership Interest, Operational Control and/or Voting Control, directly
             or indirectly, of more than one League Team, or
         b. lend money to or guarantee the debts or obligations of more than one League
             Team, except in the case of commercial lending by a bank or similar institution.
  19.Individuals or Organizations may apply for an exception to this policy, which may be
      granted by Riot on a case-by-case basis.
                                  Multiple Sponsorships
  20.League Teams and their Owners may not sponsor other League Teams.
  21.League Teams and their Owners may not sponsor players that are eligible to compete
      against their League Team.
  22.League Teams and their Owners may apply for an exception to this policy, which may
      be granted by Riot on a case-by-case basis.

Sponsorship Policies

                            General Sponsorship Restrictions
  23.The following restrictions are applicable to all sponsorships, endorsement, advertising
      or related agreements connected with a Team (“Sponsorship Agreement”).
  24.The Prohibited Sponsorship Categories and Conditional Sponsorship Categories
      provided in Appendix B apply to all Teams competing in an official competition of LoL
      Esports. To the extent that any potential sponsor fits into more than one category, the
      most restrictive rules applicable shall apply.
  25.No Sponsorship Agreement may include any entity determined by Riot to fall within the
      Prohibited Sponsorship Categories.
  26.Sponsorship Agreements with any entity determined by Riot to fall within the
      Conditional Sponsorship Categories are prohibited at International Events, but may be
      conditionally allowed on a case-by-case basis at the local regional level, provided that
      the requirements and approval process specified by the relevant League are fulfilled.
         a. A Team in a League may be permitted to have a sponsor that is within a
             Conditional Sponsorship Category during local season play of that League, but
             cannot display or represent the sponsor at International Events.

         b. Teams will be required to produce secondary jerseys without the conditional
             category sponsor for International Events.


Sponsor Brand Integration

                                      Team Name
  27.Team names must include the team’s brand (the “Primary Brand”), and may
      additionally include up to one sponsor brand (the “Secondary Brand”).
         a. Teams may only add a Secondary Brand to their team name in between splits of
             their League.
         b. Teams may drop a Secondary Brand from their team name at any time at the
             discretion of their League.
         c. Teams may change the Secondary Brand in their team name once within any
             given twelve month period.
  28.Teams must comply with any additional team name sponsor integration rules provided
      by their League.
  29.Teams may not place separators (e.g., periods, dashes, etc.) between their Primary
      Brand and Secondary Brands in their team name.
                              Team Logo and Team-code
  30.Teams may include the Primary Brand and/or up to one Secondary Brand in their team
      logo and/or team-code. Team logos and team-codes must be approved by the League.
         a. Teams that have included the Secondary Brand in their team logo may not
             change their team logo for a period of thirty-six months from the time that the
             Secondary Brand was added.
         b. Teams that have included two brands in their team-code may not change their
             team-code for a period of twenty-four months from the time that the Secondary
             Brand was added.
         c. Teams that have included the Secondary Brand in their team-code must also
             include the Secondary Brand in their team name.
         d. Teams may obtain authorization from their League to remove a brand from their
             team logo and/or team-code at any time.
                      Sponsorship Acknowledgment Agreement
  31.Prior to receiving approval for a naming sponsor, Teams and the naming sponsor must
      sign a sponsorship acknowledgment agreement provided by Riot, which, among other
      things, licenses Riot to use the sponsor’s name, brand and other related elements in
      perpetuity and obtains sponsor commitments to competitive integrity and other
      safeguards.

Team Uniform Policy

  32.Teams must comply with these uniform policies (i) when competing at International
      Events (i.e. FST, MSI, and Worlds) including during media obligations and event
      photography, and (ii) when competing in a League, unless otherwise stated in the
      League rules.
         a. Teams that fail to produce a compliant uniform by the deadline for an event may
             be subject to ad-hoc uniform modifications or other penalties.
         b. When competing in a League, Teams must comply with any additional
             restrictions provided by the rules of their League. If applicable, these additional
             restrictions will also apply when competing at an International Event.
         c. Leagues may choose to opt out of this team uniform policy for their member
             Teams for official regional league matches.
                                        Team Uniform
  33.Team uniforms consist of the following parts: (i) the team jersey, (ii) legwear, (iii) shoes,
      and (iv) other team uniform items.
         a. The team jersey may include short and long sleeve jerseys.
         b. The team legwear must be pants. Shorts are prohibited.
         c. Only closed-toe shoes are allowed.
         d. The team uniform may include over-jersey apparel such as warmups, hoodies,
             or jackets.
         e. Players may not wear hats or other headwear inside the match area, unless
             approved by competition Officials.
  34.All players on a Team must wear matching team uniforms during the match days and
      other times required by Riot.
  35.The team jersey must include (i) the Team’s logo within the chest area, and (ii) the
      player’s Summoner Name on the upper back.
         a. The team logo and the player’s Summoner Name name must be large enough to
             be easily visible on broadcast and distinguishable from sponsor logos and other
             elements on the team jersey.

                            Team Uniform Sponsor Integration
  36.Sponsor logos are permitted to be placed on the team uniform.
         a. There are no restrictions regarding the number of sponsor logos and the logo
             placement area.
         b. Teams may choose how team and sponsor logos are distributed across the
             team uniform.
         c. Teams should refrain from creating the impression that a sponsor is the Team's
             naming sponsor if this is not the case.
         d. Teams may not advertise sponsors within the Prohibited Sponsorship Categories
             (Appendix B) on their team uniform during any official competition.
         e. Teams may not advertise sponsors within the Conditional Sponsorship
             Categories (Appendix B) on their team uniform during any International Event.
                                           Riot IP
  37.To use any Riot marks or other intellectual property (IP), Teams must both (a) obtain
      express written permission from Riot, and (b) comply with any IP guidelines and/or
      instructions provided by Riot. This includes, but is not limited to: Riot Games logo,
      event logos, League logos, or League of Legends IP (such as role icons, champion art,
      etc.).

Third-Party and Riot Event Policy

  38.Professional League Teams and their players may be permitted to engage and
      participate in third party events provided that the participation complies with third-party
      event guidelines provided by Riot.
  39.Professional League Teams or their players participating in non-Riot sanctioned events
      without written approval from Riot shall be considered a breach of these LoL Esports
      Global Policies.
  40.Semi-professional League Teams must comply with the third-party event guidelines
      provided by their respective Leagues.

Unique Team-code Policy

                                        Team-Codes
  41.Team-codes may be any combination of two, three, or four characters (letters and
      numbers only).
         a. Team-codes must be unique across LoL Esports. Flexibility in uniqueness of
             team-codes may be granted for lower levels of competition.

       b. Team-codes may not be any of the protected or restricted team-codes listed on
           the LoL Esports GCD.
42.Leagues may permit an organization to use the same team-code between their main
    team and academy team at the discretion of the League.
43.Riot may deny the use of a team-code at its sole discretion.
                                    Protected Status
44.Any team that has used a team-code in a Professional League shall have its team-code
    treated as though the team is still active (i.e. protected status) for a period of one year
    from its last professional appearance.
       a. At its sole discretion, Riot may designate a team-code as “Officially Retired”,
           which grants such team-code global permanent protection.
       b. Any team that changes its team-code shall immediately lose ownership of the
           team-code that is being abandoned.
                                   Conflict Resolution
45.At its sole discretion, Riot shall resolve conflicts between two Teams using the same
    team-code.
       a. If two Teams with identical-codes are entering play at the same time, but one is
           entering a Professional League and the other is entering at a lower tier of
           competition, the Team entering the Professional League will be granted the
           team-code.
       b. If two Teams with identical team-codes are entering the same level of play at
           approximately the same time, then the Teams should work together with a
           League Official to find a compromise. If no compromise can be found, then the
           team-code will be disallowed for both Teams, and both shall be required to
           select a new team-code that conforms with this policy.

              Section C: Roster Construction Rules
Player Eligibility

                                        Minimum Age
  46.Players must meet the below minimum age requirements to compete in an official
      competition:
          a. Leagues. Players must meet the minimum age requirement of the League where
              they have registered to compete.
          b. International Events. Players must be at least seventeen years old by the start
              date of the International Event (i.e., born on or before the date seventeen years
              prior). Players must also meet the minimum age requirement of the League from
              which their Team earned qualification into the International Event.
                                   Passport Requirement
  47.Players competing in a League are required to hold a passport which is valid for a
      minimum of twelve months at all times. Exceptions may be granted at the discretion of
      the League.
                                   Single Team Exclusivity
  48.Players may not compete for more than one Team simultaneously, and cannot be
      registered to the roster of more than one Team.
                                     No Riot Employees
  49.Esports Professionals may not be simultaneously employed by Riot Games, Inc. or
      League of Legends Esports Federation LLC or any of their respective affiliates, while
      also being employed by a Team.

Player Residency

                                      Residency Status
  50.A player may obtain residency for a Region (“Resident”) by meeting any of the following
      criteria for one of its constituent countries (each, a “Residency Criteria”) :
          a. The player is a citizen or holds additional citizenship rights (such as those of the
              EU), or
          b. The player is a lawful permanent resident, or
          c. The player holds another special status (e.g., refugee or asylum status), or
          d. The player was granted a legacy exception to be considered a Resident, which
              may be discretionarily accepted or rejected by Riot.




                                                                                               11

<!-- página 12 -->
  51.When registering into a LoL Esports League for the first time, players must declare their
      residency by submitting an eligibility form to their respective League, accompanied by
      proof that they meet at least one of the Residency Criteria.
  52.A player may only be a Resident of one Region at a time.
                                   Changing Residency
  53.Players may request to change their residency to another Region if all of the cumulative
      criteria listed below are met:
         a. The player meets at least one of the Residency Criteria for the other Region, and
         b. At least twenty-four months have elapsed since the player most recently
             declared or changed their residency, and
         c. The player may apply to change their residency only between splits, i.e., players
             cannot change their residency status during an active split. For clarity, players
             qualified to an international event also cannot change their residency status until
             the event is completed.
  54.Leagues may discretionarily accept or reject a player’s request to change their
      residency.

Roster Requirements

  55.Teams must comply with these roster requirements at all times during the Season,
      unless otherwise stated in the rules of their League.
                                        Roster Size
  56.Teams are required to have a minimum of five eligible players on their competitive roster
      for official matches in their League. Teams must adhere to any specific roster size limits
      set by their League's rules.
                              Regional Roster Requirements
  57.Teams must maintain at least three players on their Starting Lineup who are Residents
      of their League’s Region at all times during the Season.
         a. Leagues may impose additional and stricter local representation rules, which will
             apply at all times during the Season.
         b. Each League’s local representation rules will also apply to that League’s Teams
             during International Events, unless otherwise stated in the rules specific to the
             International Events.
         c. Any player substitution of the Team’s Starting Lineup must comply with these
             region representation requirements and local representation rules.

                             Coaches and General Managers
  58.Teams must satisfy the following requirements on the minimum and maximum number
      of coaches:
         a. International Events and Professional Leagues. Teams must have a minimum
             of one coach and may register up to four coaches. Teams are required to
             designate one of their coaches as the “Head Coach” who is responsible for
             communicating with players during the pick and ban phase, and submitting the
             Team’s starting roster prior to official matches.
         b. Other Leagues and Competitions. Teams must comply with any requirements
             specified in the rules for the relevant League or competition.
  59.Teams must maintain a member on their staff that is responsible for administering Team
      commitments associated with official competition activities such as, content requests,
      official competition schedules, on-site studio coordination, and update calls with a
      competition representative (the “General Manager”). The General Manager is required
      to travel with the Team to all relevant official competitions, both domestically and
      internationally.
  60.Coaches and General Managers are only allowed to work for one Team at a time.
                                       Work Eligibility
  61.Teams are responsible for ensuring that their players and coaches are compliant with
      relevant local laws and are eligible to participate in all relevant competitions.
  62.Teams must comply with any work eligibility requirements provided by their League.

Team Roster Changes

                             General Roster Change Policies
  63.Teams are authorized to modify their rosters by either:
         a. transferring or trading players with other Teams,
         b. signing (or releasing) players that are not subject to a valid contract and meet
             the eligibility requirements of the Team’s League (“Free Agents”),
         c. loaning players with other Teams in accordance with player loan rules provided
             by the relevant Leagues, or
         d. using other methods established by the Team’s League.
  64.All changes to a Team’s roster must comply with all applicable player, coach, or roster
      requirements.
  65.All changes to a Team’s roster must be approved by the Team’s League before
      becoming effective.

  67.Any roster change which violates any provision of an applicable contract shall be

                                      Player Transfers
  68.Players may be transferred or traded between Teams, including across different

         b. When transferring or trading players or coaches, Teams must comply with

Roster Locks

                                  Regional Roster Locks
  69.Riot, or the entity operating an official competition, may designate periods of time
      where Teams participating in the official competition are prohibited from making any
      new roster changes (“Roster Lock”).
  70.Teams may be permitted to drop players or coaches during a Roster Lock provided that
      (i) the Team remains compliant with all applicable roster requirements, and (ii) the drop
      is approved by the competition Officials.
                                    Global Lock Period
  71.During the period of time between (i) the start date of the split three Roster Lock set by
      a Team’s League, and (ii) the Global Free Agency Date (defined below) (the “Global
      Lock Period”), no new oral or verbal commitment, financial arrangement, or contractual
      arrangement for players and coaches (including trades) will be accepted.
         a. The Global Free Agency Date for players is the third Tuesday in November each
             year (00:00, UTC). The Global Free Agency Date for coaches is the day after the
             World Championship Finals (00:00, UTC).
         b. Any contract that was signed during the Global Lock Period will not be
             approved.
         c. At the discretion of their Leagues, Teams may be permitted to drop players and
             coaches during the Global Lock Period, provided that the Team complies with all
             applicable roster requirements at all times during the Season.
         d. To maintain a reasonable level of flexibility, the Global Lock Period does not
             apply to:

Roster Emergency Policy

                                           Definition
  72.“Roster Emergency” is a situation where a team cannot compete with the full roster
      submitted before the event’s roster lock date. Examples include, but are not limited to,
      visa issues, travel restrictions, illness, or suspensions.
  73.“Emergency Substitutions” refer to adding an Emergency Substitute to the team’s
      active roster or starting lineup after the event roster lock date.
  74.“Registered Team Staff” includes head coach, assistant coach, analysts, or other staff
      listed in the Global Contract Database and registered for the event.
                                     Process & Guidelines
  75.Timeliness. Teams must report potential Roster Emergencies as soon as they become
      aware of them. Delays may lead to serious penalties.
  76.Proof & Evidence. Teams are responsible for providing clear and credible evidence of
      the emergency.
  77.Eligibility Determination. Riot will decide at its sole discretion whether the reported
      situation qualifies as a Roster Emergency. Only verified emergencies will trigger
      emergency substitutions or alternative solutions. In general, emergencies must be
      unpredictable and unavoidable. Situations that could reasonably have been planned for
      or prevented may not qualify.
  78.Notification to Opponent. Riot will notify the opposing team(s) about any verified
      emergencies (excluding private or personal details) as soon as possible to minimize
      competitive impact.
  79.Intentional Abuse. Providing false information or intentionally abusing this policy will
      result in severe penalties, including suspensions, disqualification, or monetary penalties.
                                      General Principles
  80.Roster Requirements. All standard roster requirements still apply, including minimum
      salary, age, residency, and signed eligibility form, unless explicitly approved by Riot.

   81.Eligibility. Emergency substitutions may be utilized before an existing substitutes on the
       active roster, unless additional requirements are imposed by Riot at its sole discretion.
   82.Coverage Period. Once the original player is cleared to return, they must immediately
       replace the Emergency Substitute. At that point, the Emergency Substitute will be
       removed from the team’s roster.
   83.Player Availability. Emergency Substitutes must be present at the venue and ready to
       compete at Riot’s designated time. Late arrival may result in forfeiture.
   84.Regional Restrictions. In regional leagues, teams must comply with any additional
       rules or guidelines established by the respective league.
   85.Riot Intervention. Riot will only intervene if a player’s personal health situation poses a
       serious and uncontrollable risk to others or the event.
                                Eligible Emergency Substitutes
Qualifying one of the types below may be eligible for emergency substitutes:
   86.Contracted Players. Players already contracted with the affected team and registered
       in the Global Contract Database before the roster lock date are eligible to serve as
       emergency substitutes. This includes:
          a. Players listed on the team’s current regional league roster
          b. Players from the team’s Riot-recognized affiliated Tier 2 roster
   87.Non-Tier 1 Players. Players who have not played in a Tier 1 league game during the
       active split (i.e., 0% game participation) are eligible to be emergency substitutes. Active
       split is defined as the on-going split or the split that qualifies teams to the international
       event. This may include:
          a. Free agents
          b. Substitute players of other Tier 1 teams
          c. Tier 2 players from non-affiliated teams
   88.Registered Team Staff. Coaches, analysts and other team members officially
       registered in the event roster of the affected team may also be eligible.
                                  Non-Applicable Situations
   89.Team Representation. Emergency Substitutions will no longer apply if a team is unable
       to field a starting lineup with at least three players from its original roster 72 hours
       before the first match of the event starts, unless decided otherwise by Riot at their sole
       discretion. In such cases, Riot may declare the team forfeited and, if the situation
       happens before the event begins, may allow the next eligible team to take their place.
   90.In-Game Substitutions. Player substitutions are not allowed once a game has started.
       If a player experiences an issue during the game, the team may pause the game once

     to allow a referee to assess the situation. If the player is unable to continue, the game

                                 Head Coach Emergencies
  91.In the case of a verified emergency, teams may replace their head coach with another
      team staff member who is registered for the event. Riot will review each case
      individually and determine an appropriate solution at its sole discretion.

Global Contract Database

  92.The LoL Esports League-recognized global contract database (Global Contract
      Database, or “GCD”) is a public-facing team roster list that includes the contract
      end-dates of registered players and coaches.
  93.Players and coaches on Teams in Professional Leagues must be registered to their
      Team on the GCD in order to be eligible to participate in official matches.
  94.Players and coaches must meet the pass through terms for contracts (explained below)
      in order to be eligible to be registered on the GCD. Team continuity protections only
      apply to players and coaches on the GCD.
  95.Teams must submit all required documents and comply with all GCD registration
      procedures provided by their League.

Contract Requirements and Pass Through Terms

                            Appendix E: Player Loan Policy
1. Background and Purpose
       a. The Player Loan Policy is drafted to support a healthy competitive environment
           by facilitating player development and mobility. Each League may establish rules
           under this policy to ensure fair, transparent loans that prioritize player welfare
           and maintain competitive balance.
2. Definitions
       a. “Loan Agreement” means a written agreement that documents a Player Loan
           transaction.
       b. “Loaned Player” means a player who is loaned or temporarily transferred from
           one team to another team for a specific period of time.
       c. “Loaning Team” means a team that temporarily transfers or loans out a player
           from its official roster to another team for a specific period of time.
       d. “Player Loan” means a transaction in which a team temporarily transfers or
           loans a player registered on its roster to another team for a specific period of
           time and during which the player is eligible to compete for the receiving team.
       e. “Receiving Team” means a team that receives a player who is transferred or
           loaned from another team for a specific period.
       f. “Season” means the period between two consecutive Global Free Agency
           Dates.
3. Eligible Players for Loans
       a. Teams can only loan out players who are on their official roster, as further
           defined by the applicable regional League.
       b. A Loaned Player, whilst being on the official team roster, cannot be loaned or
           traded from the Receiving Team to another team again during the loan period
           contract (the former is considered as “Sub-loan”).
       c. If a player has been loaned for two Seasons (six splits) in any League
           consecutively, the player will enter into a one split cooldown period in which the
           player cannot be loaned.
       d. Loaned players must satisfy local player eligibility requirements in the jurisdiction
           where the Receiving Team operates in order to be registered. This is the same
           requirement that applies to all other players.


4. Loan Parties Restrictions
       a. Loans between Tier 1 teams are strictly prohibited. Any player who has
           participated in one or more official Tier 1 matches for their Parent Team during


                                                                                             29

<!-- página 30 -->
          the most recent split cannot not be loaned to another Tier 1 team during the
          current loan window.
      b. Within the same region, loans may only occur across tiers. Specifically, loans are
          permitted between a professional (Tier 1) league and its associated Tier 2
          league(s), in either direction. Loans between tier 2 teams within the same region
          are prohibited.
      c. Cross-regional loans are permitted only if the receiving team competes in a Tier
          2 league. Additional restrictions may be imposed by regional leagues.
      d. A player loaned to a Tier 2 team can only participate in Tier 2 competitions for
          the entire duration of the loan.
      e. Loans are permitted only between two teams that are officially recognized within
          their respective regions. A single loan agreement involving more than two teams
          (e.g., three-way or multi-party loan arrangements) is strictly prohibited.


5. Loan Slots
      a. A team can have a maximum of 2 players loaned in AND out at any given time.
          The team cannot loan in/out additional players until one of the loan agreements
          expires or is terminated if the threshold is met.
      b. For competitive reasons, a team cannot loan 2 of their players to the same team,
          or similarly receive 2 Loaned Players from the same team.
      c. The Loaned Player will take up both roster slots of the Loaning Team and
          Receiving Team. The Loaned Player will be considered inactive in the Loaning
          Team roster and active in the Receiving Team roster. The Loaned Player should
          be marked as “Loaned Player” in the roster of the Receiving Team on the Global
          Contract Database (“GCD”).


6. Loan Window and Duration
      a. The applicable League Entity will only accept loan applications during the
          Off-seasons while the regional league is not active. Loans cannot happen during
          the Global Lock Period.
      b. The maximum length of a loan agreement is one Season, and the minimum
          length is one split. The length of loan agreement must extend through the end of
          the competitive split of the Receiving Team, including until the end of the
          upcoming international event if the Receiving Team is qualified.
            i.   To further clarify, a loan agreement has to start AND end during the
                  Off-seasons, while it can also end before the Global Free Agency but

                  after the team has completed all of local and international matches of
                  that Season.
      c. Loan extension is allowed provided that the total loan duration does not exceed
          the maximum length requirement. Discussion of loan extension can happen
          anytime during the loan period, but the extension application will only be
          accepted during Off-seasons.
      d. The Loaned Player cannot be called back to the Loaning Team during a single
          split, unless otherwise agreed by the League Entity in its absolute discretion with
          the prior consent of the Loaning Team, Receiving Team and Loaned Player.
          Callback will only be permitted in extreme circumstances.


7. Requirements on Loan Agreements
      a. All Player Loan transactions must be documented with a written Loan
          Agreement. The League Entity has created a form that can be optionally used by
          teams and players for this purpose. However, all Loan Agreements must specify
          with clarity the rights and obligations of the Loaning Team, Receiving Team, and
          the Loaned Player. Each Loan Agreement must, at a minimum, include terms
          that define the loan duration, financial arrangements, termination conditions and
          other terms that are required by the League and/or local legal regulations. To be
          more specific:
            i.   Loan Duration
                     1. The Loan Agreement must explicitly state the start and end date
                         of the loan. The duration of a Player Loan cannot extend beyond
                         the Loaned Player’s contract length with the Loaning Team.
                     2. After the loan period, the Loaned Player will continue the original
                         Player Services Agreement with the Loaning Team. The original
                         agreement with the Loaning Team cannot be automatically

                         are observed, and (b) all of the financial terms are included in the
                         text of the Loan Agreement and not in separate side letters or
                         other documents.
                     2. There is no restriction on which team (the Loaning Team or the
                         Receiving Team) pays the salary of the Loaned Player during the
                         loan period, provided that conflict of interest rules are observed.
