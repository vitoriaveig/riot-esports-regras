                              VALORANT Champions Tour
                               Roster Construction Rules

Last Updated: August 21, 2024

Introduction

Background. VALORANT Champions Tour LLC, a Delaware limited liability company
(“League Entity”), along with its affiliates, organizes a series of regional, cross-regional, and
international esports tournaments and related media events under the name VALORANT
Champions Tour (the “VCT”) which feature competitive play (both live and online) of the video
game VALORANT (the “Game”) by professional esports players and teams. The following are
each a “VCT League” or “League”: (i) VCT Americas, (ii) VCT Pacific, and (iii) VCT EMEA.

Purpose. The League Entity has developed these Roster Construction Rules (“Roster
Construction Rules”) to standardize practices and procedures that apply to team rosters and
player movement and thereby increase opportunities across national boundaries so that players in
one country or region who have achieved a certain level of success in the Game can compete on
an equal footing with players at the same level of accomplishment in other countries and regions.
By adhering to these rules, we can provide a world-class competitive experience that upholds the
highest standards of esports.

Acknowledgment and Acceptance. These Roster Construction Rules apply to and are binding
on all esports team-owning entities that participate in the VCT (“Team Entity”) for the 2025
Season. All the provisions of these Roster Construction Rules are subject to the terms and
conditions of the applicable Team Participation Agreement between the Team Entity and the
League Entity (“TPA”) and shall be interpreted in accordance therewith. Capitalized terms in
these Roster Construction Rules, unless expressly defined otherwise herein, shall have the
meaning ascribed to such terms in the TPA.

         below for the VCT Masters and Champions events (collectively, “International
         Competitions”). Such International Competition Roster must form an eligible

1.6.     Hub City Requirements.

       1.6.1.   Housing. Each Team Entity must enter into an Esports Services
                Agreement with its Team Members and Head Coach, in accordance with
                Exhibit A attached hereto. In an Esports Services Agreement, the parties
                are free to negotiate the business terms, including the terms applicable to
                housing. At a minimum, each Team Entity must provide reasonable
                support to its Team Members in the housing search process. Other housing
                options that may be mutually agreed upon include a residential team
                house, individual apartments, or a housing stipend.

       1.6.2.   Training Facility. Each Team Entity is responsible for providing a
                training facility for its Team Members for the full duration of the Season.
                Each training facility must, at a minimum, include enough sets of the
                following to cover five (5) Team Members on the Active Roster and the
                Head Coach: PC, monitor, monitor arm (optional), desk, chair, headset,
                in-ear headphones, and peripherals. Training facilities must be able to
                support online play for League Games, if necessary.

       1.6.3.   Hub City Eligibility. Each Team Entity is responsible for obtaining all
                visas, passports, vaccination documentation and work permits that are
                required for its Team Members and Head Coach to travel to-and-from the
                Hub City and to provide services from the Hub City during the Season. It
                is the Team Entity’s sole responsibility to obtain and have available when
                necessary the appropriate valid travel and work documents for its Team
                Members, Head Coach and any other players, coaches, administrators,
                analysts, contractors, consultants, employees, staff and other personnel
                (the foregoing collectively, “Team Personnel”) whose services are
                required by the Team Entity in the Hub City.

                     with a multi-Season term, that agreement must expire automatically at the
                     end of the last Season of such agreement so that the player is free to make
                     a move during the off-Season. A Team Entity may enter into an Esports
                     Services Agreement with a player that extends beyond the term of the
                     Team Entity's TPA provided that all such Esports Services Agreements
                     must include an option to terminate the Esports Services Agreement on the
                     part of either party in the event Team Entity’s TPA with League Entity is
                     terminated, expires, or is not renewed for any reason.

                     sign with a Team during a VCT Transfer Window but may be moved from
                     the Reserve Roster (as defined below) to the Active Roster at any time,
                     subject to compliance with Section 4.1.2 below.

2.2.     Regional Residency Requirement

       2.2.1.   Regional Representation. Each team that participates in the VCT
                pursuant to a valid TPA (“VCT Participating Team”) will be assigned to
                a League. In order to encourage the training and development of players in
                each League Territory and to maintain the regional identity of the VCT
                Participating Teams that compete in a global competition and to encourage
                the type of regional identification that is important to fans and sponsors,
                each Team Entity must maintain on the Starting Roster (as defined below),
                at all times during the Season, at least four (4) Team Members who are
                Residents (as defined below) of a country in the applicable League
                Territory. The Leagues of the VCT are: (i) Americas, (ii) Europe, Middle
                East and Africa (“EMEA”), (iii) Asia-Pacific (“Pacific”), and (iv) China.
                The countries inside each of these geographic regions are a “League
                Territory”.

       2.2.2.   Resident Defined; Certification and Proof of Residency. A Team
                Member is considered a “Resident” of a country within a League
                Territory if the Team Member is either (i) a citizen of a country in the
                League Territory or holds additional citizenship rights (such as those of
                the EU) for that League Territory, (ii) a lawful permanent resident, or (iii)
                the holder of other special status (e.g., refugee or asylum status) in a
                country in the League Territory. Each Team Member must certify his/her
                residency by (a) submitting an eligibility form and (b) providing proof that
                the Team Member holds one of the residency statuses set forth in causes
                (i)-(iii) above.

       2.2.3.   Single Residency Status. For purposes of determining Residency and
                compliance with the Rules, a Team Member may only be a Resident of
                one country at a time.

       2.2.4.   Dual Citizens. A Team Member who has dual citizenship or otherwise
                meets the qualifications to be considered a Resident of more than one

3.   Roster Requirements
     3.1.   Purpose. The rules in this Section 3 are designed to ensure that all teams have a
            balanced and consistent number of players and coaches, preventing any team from
            gaining an unfair advantage.

     3.2.   Roster Enforcement Period. The Team Entity is required to maintain, at all
            times between the Start of Season until the End of Season (the “VCT
            Competitive Season”), a Minimum Roster (as defined in Section 3.5) and have a
            designated General Manager (as defined in Section 3.4) and at least one Head
            Coach (as defined in Section 3.3), in accordance with league requirements.

     3.3.   Coaches. The Team Entity is required to maintain, at all times during the Season,
            a minimum of one and a maximum of three designated coaches (“Coaches”) who
            will be considered official coaches for the Team. The Team Entity is required to
            designate one of its Coaches as the head coach (the “Head Coach”) who will be
            listed in the Global Contract Database.

     3.4.   General Manager. The Team Entity is required to maintain, at all times during
            the Season, one designated Team staff member (the “General Manager”) who is
            responsible for administering Team commitments associated with weekly League
            activities including, but not limited to, content requests, League Game schedule,
            on-site studio coordination and weekly update calls with a League player
            management representative. This same person will be required to travel with the
            Team to all Covered Events. Teams are permitted to designate their Head Coach
            or other Coach as the General Manager.

     3.5.   Roster Size. The Team Entity is required to maintain, at all times during the VCT
            Competitive Season, a total competitive roster of no less than five (5) Team
            Members (“Minimum Roster”), each of whom is eligible to participate in League
            Games (the “Active Roster”), and no more than ten (10) Team Members (the
            “Maximum Roster”), which includes the Reserve Roster (as defined in Section
            3.6).

     3.6.   Reserve Roster. The Team Entity may hold up to five (5) reserve players who are
            (i) in the process of becoming eligible to join the Active Roster (“Reserves”), but
            who do not meet the qualifications to join the Active Roster (“Reserve Roster”),
            (ii) a Loaned Player, or (iii) a Two-Way Player. Collectively, the Active Roster
            and Reserve Roster shall be referred to as the “Full Team Roster” and may not
            fall below the Minimum Roster requirement during the Season or exceed the
            Maximum Roster requirement at any time.

               ● Note: Examples of Reserves include players who have applied for, but not
                 yet received, a visa which will grant them work eligibility, players who do

      3.7.   Starting Roster. The term “Starting Roster” means the five (5) Team Members

             Team Member who is not a Resident of a country in the applicable League
             Territory).

             an Esports Services Agreement with the Team Entity they are playing for. Each
             Head Coach must have an Esports Services Agreement with the Team Entity they
             are coaching for.

      3.9.   Pass Through Terms. All Esports Services Agreements must comply with the
             requirements of Exhibit A attached hereto.

     3.10.   Single Team Exclusivity. Coaches and General Managers are only allowed to
             work for one VCT Team at a time. Team Members will not be allowed to compete
             for more than one VCT Team simultaneously and therefore cannot be listed on the
             Full Team Roster of more than one VCT Team, but playing for a Non-IL Team (as
             defined in Section 7 below) in accordance with the Loaned and Two-Way Player
             rules set forth herein shall not be a violation of this rule.

     3.11.   Work Eligibility. The Team Entity shall be fully responsible at all times during
             the Season for ensuring that its Team Personnel are compliant with relevant local
             laws and eligible to participate in all VCT competitions, both in the Hub City and
             internationally, including obtaining all required passports, visas and other legal
             documentation. The Team Entity shall promptly submit documentation and proof
             of compliance to League Entity upon request. Failure to comply with this Section
             3.11 may result in Disciplinary Action, and players without proper documentation
             may be ruled ineligible to compete.

      4.1.   General Roster Change Rules. The Team Entity is authorized to use two
             methods to make changes to its Full Team Roster: (1) trading players with other
             VCT Participating Teams; and (2) signing (or releasing) Free Agents. The Team
             Entity is prohibited from using loan methods to temporarily trade a player to
             another VCT Participating Team. No trade is allowed to result in a player having

4.1.1.   Full Roster Requirement. No change to the Full Team Roster shall
         relieve a Team Entity of the requirement to maintain a Minimum Roster
         during the VCT Competitive Season. If, at any point during the VCT
         Competitive Season, a Team’s Active Roster falls below the Minimum
         Roster, the Team Entity will be subject to Disciplinary Action, unless the
         League Entity gives permission to drop below the minimum roster
         requirement. Permission to drop below the Minimum Roster will be for a
         limited period specified by the League Entity, may be subject to conditions
         imposed by the League Entity and may be given or withheld at the
         discretion of the League Entity.

4.1.2.   Reserve Roster Change Submission Deadline. If the Team Entity wishes
         to have a player on its Reserve Roster moved to its Active Roster, the
         Team Entity must notify the League Entity of the status change at least
         one day before the Team’s first match in the week in order for the player
         to be eligible to compete in any League Games for that week. A Team
         Member will not be allowed to play in a League Game unless the Team
         Entity has provided the League Entity with all required documentation and
         ensured compliance with work eligibility requirements.

4.1.3.   League Approval. Changes to the Team’s roster require the approval of
         the League Entity to ensure compliance with these Roster Construction
         Rules. The League Entity may deny any request to add or remove a Team
         Member from the Team’s roster if, in the judgment of the League Entity,
         the Team Entity or the Team Member has failed to comply with these
         Roster Construction Rules or other league Rules.

4.1.4.   Competitive Disclosures – No Roster Surprises. If a roster change
         request is submitted by a Team Entity to the League Entity for approval
         but the League Entity has not yet granted or denied the requested approval
         before a League Game is scheduled to commence, the League Entity may
         (but is not required to) inform other Teams of the existence of a roster
         change request (including the identity of players involved) so that the
         change does not have an adverse impact on the opposing Team’s
         preparations for competition.

4.1.5.   Transfer Windows. Roster additions may be made effective as early as
         the Transfer Window Start and must become effective no later than the
         Transfer Window Close, as explained in greater detail below. The terms
         “Transfer Window Start” and “Transfer Window Close” shall have the
         meanings assigned to them in Section 1.2 above.

4.2.      Team Member Trades. During the VCT Transfer Window, the Team Entity may

          Teams, including between VCT Leagues, provided that the details of the trade,

          review and approval in accordance with Section 5 below. The League Entity’s
          oversight of Team Member trades helps to ensure that players are treated fairly
          and that their rights and interests are safeguarded throughout the Season.

        4.2.1.   Quantity. There is no maximum total number of Team Members who may
                 be traded per Season.

        4.2.2.   Asymmetrical Trades. Trades are not required to be structured as
                 symmetrical exchanges (e.g., 1-for-1, 2-for-2). Trade transactions are not
                 limited to two VCT Participating Teams per transaction.

                     ● Note: A trade deal may include players from two or more VCT
                       Participating Teams. For example, a situation in which team A
                       trades player X to team B, team B trades player Y to team C, and
                       team C trades player Z to team A.

        4.2.3.   Trades for Cash. Players may be traded from one Team to another in
                 exchange for a non-contingent payment of cash or other consideration
                 subject to the conditions and requirements set forth in the Rules. Hybrid
                 transactions, where a player is traded in exchange for a combination of
                 one or more players plus cash or other consideration, are also permitted.

        4.2.4.   Trades Between VCT Leagues. Players from one VCT League may be
                 traded for any players from another VCT League, subject to the eligibility

4.5.     Player Loans. A VCT Participating Team (“Loaning Team”) may transfer a
         Team Member on its Full Team Roster (“Loaned Player”) to another team that is
         not a VCT Participating Team (the “Receiving Team”) on a temporary basis in
         accordance with the rules in this Section. These rules are designed to ensure a fair
         and transparent process for loaning players, while prioritizing the players’ welfare
         and maintaining competitive balance within the VCT.

       4.5.1.   Eligibility Requirements. Any Team Member may be loaned or
                transferred on a temporary basis if the Team Member: (a) is listed in the
                Loaning Team’s Full Team Roster; (b) has entered in to an ESA with the
                Loaning Team in compliance with the Rules; and (c) has consented to the
                loan, as provided in Section 4.5.4 below.

       4.5.2.   Restrictions on Player Loans. A Team Entity may not, at any time, loan
                more than two (2) of its players to other team(s). A Team Entity may not
                loan a particular player more than twice during a season. Loans can be
                made at any time during the VCT Transfer Window. A player loan cannot
                extend beyond the last day of the player’s ESA. If the ESA expires or
                terminates for any reason, the loan must terminate at the same time.

       4.5.3.   Loan Agreements. All player loan transactions must be documented with
                a written loan agreement (“Loan Agreement”). The League Entity has
                created a form that can be used by teams and players for this purpose. Use
                of the League Entity’s form is entirely optional. However, all Loan
                Agreements must specify the rights and obligations of the Loaning Team,
                Receiving Team, and the Loaned Player. In particular, each Loan
                Agreement must: (a) explicitly state the start and end date of the loan; (b)
                provide termination rights for both teams; (c) expressly state the rights (if
                any) that the Loaning Team may have to terminate the Loan Agreement
                for convenience and call the Loaned Player back to the Loaning Team; (d)
                specify the notice required for any termination for convenience; (e)
                contain an acknowledgement from the Loaning Team and the Receiving
                Team that the VCT’s conflict of interest rules apply to the player loan; and
                (f) provide that the League Entity is a third party beneficiary with

         independent rights to enforce the provisions of the Loan Agreement
         relating to conflicts of interest.

4.5.4.   Player Consent. The Loaning Team is responsible for ensuring that player
         consent to the transaction is obtained. The Loaned Player may consent to a
         loan either in advance before any loan has been negotiated or at the time
         of the loan. Advance consent may be given in the text of the ESA. If the
         Loaned Player signs the Loan Agreement, the Loaning Team will have
         satisfied its obligation to obtain player consent. Player consent is required
         for the extension of an existing loan.

4.5.5.   Roster Rules. A Loaned Player will be moved to the Loaning Team’s
         Reserve Roster for the duration of the Loan Agreement. The Loaned
         Player will remain on the Loaning Team’s Full Team Roster while loaned
         and will be counted against the Loaning Team’s Maximum Roster size.

4.5.6.   League Approval. Each Loan Agreement, as well as any amendments or
         modifications to an existing Loan Agreement, must be submitted by the
         Team Entity in advance, in writing, for review and written approval by the
         League Entity before becoming effective. Such review and approval of the
         Loan Agreement enables the League Entity to ensure that (a) the
         obligations of the parties are clearly stated; (b) player rights are respected;
         and (c) the rules for player loans in this Section are uniformly observed.
         The League Entity shall provide an initial response to a request for
         approval of a Loan Agreement (or amendments thereto) within five (5)
         business days of receipt, provided that such response may be a request for
         additional information, and League Entity shall be permitted to take as
         much time as reasonably necessary to fully review and consider the terms
         of the agreement or amendment before making a final determination.

4.5.7.   Conflicts of Interest. A player loan transaction can create conflicts of
         interest for both the Loaning Team and Receiving Team. For example, a
         conflict of interest is created (a) if a Receiving Team joins a VCT League
         during the term of a Loan Agreement, or (b) in any situation where a
         Loaned Player is required to compete against the player’s Loaning Team.
         The Loaning Team is responsible for avoiding conflicts of interest. Each
         Loan Agreement must include a process for terminating the Loan
         Agreement to resolve the conflict of interest. The Loaned Player will be
         ineligible to compete in a VCT League until the conflict of interest is
         resolved.

4.6.     Two-Way Players. The Team Entity may designate Team Members as Two-Way
         Players as follows:

       4.6.1.   Two-Way Player Defined. The term “Two-Way Player” means a player
                who is subject to a valid written Esports Services Agreement with a VCT
                Participating Team who is eligible to compete in both a VCT League for
                the VCT Participating Team and in another competition for a Non-IL
                Team (as defined in Section 7) affiliated with the VCT Participating Team.

       4.6.2.   League Approval. Two-Way Player status assignment requests must be
                submitted by the Team Entity in advance, in writing, for review by the
                League Entity to ensure compliance with these Roster Construction Rules.
                The League Entity will provide written confirmation of either approval or
                denial once the review is complete.

       4.6.3.   Roster Rules. Team Members designated as Two-Way Players are
                included in the Team Entity’s Full Team Roster. Two-Way Players may be
                held on either the Active Roster or Reserve Roster. To compete in a match
                of a VCT League, Two-Way Players must be on the Team Entity’s Active
                Roster.

       4.6.4.   Participation Threshold. Two-Way Players who have played in 50% or
                more VCT League matches in a competition stage (i.e., Kickoff, Stage 1
                or Stage 2) will be considered to have reached the “Participation
                Threshold”. The Participation Threshold will reset at the beginning of
                each competition stage. To help maintain competitive balance, a Non-IL
                Team may not have more than one (1) player who exceeds the
                Participation Threshold during a competition stage.

       4.6.5.   Limits on Player Movement. The VCT Team Entity may designate a
                maximum of five (5) players as Two-Way Players for a given Season.

       4.6.6.   Two-Way Player Compensation. To help ensure that similarly situated
                players are compensated in a uniform manner across Leagues, if a
                Two-Way Player has played in a VCT League match during any given
                week, then the player’s compensation for that week must comply with the
                VCT Minimum on a prorated weekly basis. If a Two-Way Player is
                reassigned to a Non-IL Team, such player may be placed on the Team’s
                Reserve Roster, and may be compensated below the VCT Minimum, but
                the player’s compensation must always comply with applicable law. The
                Team Entity must also adhere to the other requirements related to such
                arrangement as set forth in Exhibit A.

       7.4.2.   Academy Team Name. The name of the Academy Team must include the

                relegated to a Challengers League, it will then have two teams in a
                Challengers League and therefore must consolidate the two teams into a
                single team that competes at the Challengers League level.

       7.4.4.   Roster Compliance. Each Academy Team must comply with the roster
                requirements of its Challengers League, including team roster size and
                residency rules.
