1.   Introduction and Purpose

      1.1.   Background

      Riot Games Services Pte. Ltd. (“League Entity”), along with its affiliates (collectively,
      “Riot”), organizes a series of cross-regional esports tournaments and related media
      events under the name League of Legends Championship Pacific (the “LCP”) which
      feature competitive play (both live and online) of the video game League of Legends (the
      “League of Legends”) by professional esports players and teams. The League Entity
      has entered into written agreements with certain team-owning entities (each, a “Team
      Entity”) governing the right to compete in the LCP. Pursuant to the Team Participation
      Agreements (“TPA”) and as more fully described therein, the League Entity has granted
      each Team Entity the right to field a team that will compete in the LCP (“Team”).

      1.2.   Purpose

      The League Entity has created this LCP Event Specific Ruleset (the “Event Ruleset”) to
      set forth certain policies, rules, and procedures that will apply to all Teams participating
      in a specific event (i.e. Split 1, Split 2, Promotion & Relegation Tournament etc.). This
      Event Ruleset is in addition to, and not in lieu of, the LCP Official Competition Ruleset
      (the “Competition Ruleset”) and LCP Policies.

      1.3.   Acknowledgement and Acceptance

      The acknowledgement of the Competition Ruleset, and an agreement to be bound by
      and comply with their terms, is an essential term of each TPA. This Event Ruleset is the
      forms part of the broader set of rules and policies (“Rules”) referenced in the TPA. The
      League Entity may issue interpretations, guidance and other policy documents (each, a
      “Guidance Document”) from time to time in order to explain, clarify, supplement or
      provide informal guidance on the provisions of the Policies, the Competition Ruleset and
      the other Rules. All such Guidance Documents shall be deemed to be incorporated into,
      and form part of, the Competition Ruleset upon their release to the Teams.

      1.4.   Application

      Each Team Entity acknowledges and agrees that a breach of, or failure to comply with,
      the Competition Ruleset or any Guidance Document by (a) any person or entity who,
      directly or indirectly through any intermediate corporations or other entities, owns
      securities, equity interests or other ownership interests in the Team Entity or the Team
      Marks (“Owner”) shall be deemed a breach of, or failure to comply with, the Competition
      Ruleset or any Guidance Document by the Team Entity; and (b) any player, coach,
      administrator, analyst, contractor, consultant, employee, staff member or other person
      retained by the Team Entity or Owners who from time to time perform the Team Entity’s
      obligations or exercise the Team Entity’s rights under the Team Participation Agreement

2.   Overall Competition Format
      2.1.   Teams Standing Methodology

      Teams will be ranked in the Regular Season of a Split according to the number of
      Matches they win or lose (“W/L Records”).

      In the event of a tie between Teams based on W/L Records, the following tie-breaking
      mechanisms shall be utilized, in the following order of precedence, until a tie no longer
      exists:


                                                                                               4

<!-- página 5 -->
2.1.1.   1st Level: Points (“Pts”)

  The Team will receive 1 point for each game it wins, and will subtract 1 point for
  each game it loses.

  The Team with the higher number of points will rank higher.

2.1.2.   2nd Level: Head-to-Head (“H2H”)

  2-way tie: the Team that won the most number of games against the other Team
  will rank higher.

  More than 2 teams tied: The total number of games played amongst the
  tied-Teams will be considered and the Team that has the most wins will rank
  higher and be removed from the tie. The remaining Teams shall have their tie
  re-considered in the order of the tie-breaking mechanisms under this Section 2.1.

      ● Illustration:
              ○ The results of a Split are as follows:
                       ■ Match Bo3 between A vs B - 2-0
                       ■ Match Bo3 between B vs C - 2-1
                       ■ Match Bo3 between C vs A - 2-0
              ○ Total games played amongst the tied-Teams (A, B and C) will be 2
                  +3+2=7
                       ■ C will rank the highest out of the 3 Teams (3 out of 7
                          games won)
                       ■ Between A and B, the respective levels of tie-breaking
                          mechanisms under this Section 2.1 will be utilized to
                          determine a ranking

2.1.3.   3rd Level: Victory Time (“VT”)
  2-way tie: The Team with the shorter total VT against the other Team will rank
  higher.

  More than 2 teams tied: The Team with the shortest total VT will rank higher and
  be removed from the tie. The remaining Teams shall have their tie re-considered
  in the order of the tie-breaking mechanisms under this Section 2.1.

      ● Illustration:
              ○ The results of a Split are as follows:
                       ■ Match 1: A vs B - 2-0 (Total VT: 80 mins)
                       ■ Match 2: B vs C - 2-0 (Total VT: 76 mins)
                       ■ Match 3: C vs A - 2-0 (Total VT: 75 mins)
              ○ C will rank the highest out of the 3 Teams (shortest Total VT of 75
                  mins)

      7.2.     Regular Season

      The Regular Season of Split 3 will consist of 2 phases: Group Stage and Group Seeding
      Bracket. By the end of Regular Season, based on the Team Standing, the top 6 Teams
      will qualify for the Playoff of Split 3 to find three (3) best teams of LCP to Worlds.

      For the avoidance of doubt, the results of Mid Season will not carry over to Season
      Finals besides the purpose of dividing Teams into groups in Group Stage. Match results
      in Group Stage will award the Teams standing before it being reset by entering the
      Group Seeding Bracket.

      All matches in the Regular Season will be Best-of-three (BO3) matches, using Fearless
      Draft mode (as defined in the LCP Official Competition Ruleset).

              7.2.1.   Group Stage

                Based on the standing of the Split 2 Regular Season, 8 teams will be divided into
                2 groups:

                    ● Contender Group: top 4 standing teams
                    ● Breakout Group: bottom 4 standing teams

                Each group will participate in a Single Round-Robin format. Each team will be
                ranked based on the W/L record, followed by the Pts.

7.2.2.   Group Seeding Bracket

  Group Breaker: Based on the Group Stage’s result, four (4) Teams will participate
  in a cross group match(es). The match-up is:

      ● Contender Group 3rd standing vs Breakout Group 2nd standing.
      ● Contender Group 4th standing vs Breakout Group 1st standing.

  Teams from the Contender Group earn the right of side selection for this match.
  The winning Teams in these cross group matches will join the Contender Group,
  while the losing Teams will join the Breakout Group.

  For the avoidance of doubt, if a team from the Breakout Group joins the
  Contender Group, then they become a Team in the Contender Group. However,
  Teams from the Contender Group in the Group Stage are always considered as
  higher standing than teams from the Breakout Group, and this will affect their
  match-up opponent (and vice versa).

  Group Seeding Bracket (“SB”): Each group will play a Double Elimination bracket

8.2.     Semi-Professional Team Qualification

The top-performing Semi-Professional Teams from each Semi-Professional League may
be invited to participate in the LCP P&R. Semi-Professional Teams invited to the LCP
P&R will compete for an opportunity to secure a position in the LCP as a Guest Team. In
the LCP P&R in 2025, the Semi-Professional League that will have a dedicated
competition spot are: VCS, PCS, LJL.

8.3.     Wild Card Team Qualification

The best performing Wild Card team, identified as a Team from LCP Region that do not
have a dedicated Semi-Professional League within their Regions or Territories, may be
invited to the LCP P&R to compete for an opportunity to secure a position in the LCP as
a Guest Team.

8.4.     P&R Tournament Mechanism

Among the Guest Team(s) in Section 8.1 that is subject to the LCP P&R participation,
the mechanism to form a competition format is as follows:

    ● Free-for-all (“FFA”) spot: The LCP Guest Team slot allocated to the Team
       referenced in Section 8.1.1.
    ● Regional Merit spot: The LCP Guest Team slot allocated to the Team referenced
       in Section 8.1.2.

        8.4.1.   Regional Merit spot

          This spot will be contested by the top-performing Semi-Professional Team from
          the same Home Region. The winning Team of this match will secure a spot as a
          Guest Team in the LCP for the following Season, while the losing Team will return
          to the Semi-Professional League in their Home Region for the next Season.

        8.4.2.   Free-for-all (“FFA”) spot

          This spot will be contested by the remaining top-performing Semi-Professional
          Teams and WildCard Team that earn eligibility to compete in the LCP P&R. The
          winning Team from this group will secure a spot as a Guest Team in the LCP for
          the following Season, while the losing Teams will return to (1) the
          Semi-Professional League in their Home Region or (2) the WildCard Tournament
          if their Regions or Territories do not have a dedicated Semi-Professional League,
          for the following Season.

8.5.     P&R Tournament Competition Format

League Officials will announce the Guest Team(s) that have to participate in the LCP
P&R towards the end of LCP Season Finals. Since the participating Team(s) will remain
undetermined until that time, the final schedule and competition format of the LCP P&R
will not be available until the said time.

League Officials will provide a sample of the competition format as indicated below,
however the final schedule and competition format of LCP P&R will be sent to Team(s) at
least 7 days prior to the start of the LCP P&R.

For the avoidance of doubt, the LCP P&R competition format will consist of the following
structure, regardless of its actual participating Team(s):

    1. The competition format will be Single Elimination
    2. The match type will be Fearless Draft.
    3. The Guest Team from LCP will play only one (1) match against the top Domestic
        League Team coming up from the respective mechanism spots (Regional Merit
        and Free for all) and the winning Team of that Match will become/remain the LCP
        Guest Team next season.

The sample competition format is as follows, in which the LCP P&R will be divided into 2
sections. Depending on the situation, if the Regional Merit matches do not take place or
the number of participants in the Free-for-All matches changes, the format may differ
from the sample below:

    ● Regional Merit (Bo5): single elimination match between the LCP Guest Team
       from Regional Merit mechanism and the top-performing Semi-Professional Team
       from the same Home Region of the Guest Team.
    ● Free-for-all (Bo5): random draw of single elimination matches between the
       remaining top-performing Semi-Professional Teams and WildCard Team. The
       winning team from this group will play a single elimination match with the LCP
       Guest Team from Free-for-all mechanism.
