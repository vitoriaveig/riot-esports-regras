1.   Introduction and Purpose
      1.1. Background
      VALORANT Champions Tour LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (“League
      Entity”), along with its affiliates (collectively, “Riot”), organizes a series of regional,
      cross-regional, and international esports tournaments and related media events under the
      name VALORANT Champions Tour (the “VCT”) which feature competitive play (both
      live and online) of the video game VALORANT (the “Game”) by professional esports
      players and teams. The following are each a “VCT League” or “League”: (i) VCT Americas,
      (ii) VCT Pacific, (iii) VCT EMEA, and (iv) VCT China. The League Entity has entered into
      written agreements with certain team-owning entities (each, a “Team Entity”) governing
      right to compete in the VCT and their respective Leagues (each such agreement, a “Team
      Participation Agreement”). Pursuant to the Team Participation Agreements and as more
      fully described therein, the League Entity has granted each Team Entity the right to field
      a team that will compete in the VCT and its respective League (“Team”).
      The 2025 Masters Toronto event is an international esports tournament (“International
      Competition”) held as part of the VCT.

      1.2. Purpose
      The League Entity has created this Masters Toronto Event-Specific Ruleset (the
      “Event-Specific Ruleset”) to set forth certain policies, rules, and procedures that will
      apply to all Teams participating in the Masters Toronto event. This Event-Specific
      Ruleset is in addition to, and not in lieu of, the VALORANT Official Competition
      Ruleset (the “Competition Ruleset”) and the VCT25 Roster Construction Rules (the
      “RCR”).

2.   VCT Masters Toronto Event Structure
      2.1. Competitive Format
         ● 12 Teams
         ● 8 Team Swiss System Format (“Group Stage”) with the #2 and #3 seed Teams
            from the respective VCT Leagues
         ● The top four teams from the Group Stage and the four #1 seed Teams from the
            respective VCT Leagues qualify for an 8 team double-elimination bracket
            (“Playoff Stage”)
         ● All matches are best-of-three; Lower Finals and Grand Finals are best-of-five

      2.2. Seeding and Groups
      The Group Stage Draw will include the eight #2 and #3 seed Teams from the respective
      VCT Leagues who qualified for Masters Toronto. The Group Stage of Masters Toronto
      will feature a three (3) round Swiss format. Teams will need two (2) wins to progress to
      playoffs. For Round 1, teams will be sorted into four matchups with the objective of
      creating competitive, international matchups. #2 seed Teams will each face off against a
      #3 Seed from another region. Regional seeding for Masters Toronto is based on
      placement in their respective regional finals.

      Round 1 - Draw
      Teams from the same region cannot face each other in Round 1. If a team from the same
      region is drawn into the same matchup as another team from their region, they will be
      placed back into the pool after a separate, uncommon regional team is selected. If they
      were drawn into the last slot (i.e. Match 4, Slot 2), they will swap with the team
      immediately before them until a valid draw is complete.

      Pool 1 - Am #2, CN #2, EMEA #2, Pac #2 
      Pool 2 - Am #3, CN #3, EMEA #3, Pac #3 




      Round 1 - Seeding

      Seeding from regional events are only upheld for Round 1 of Group Stage. Following
      Round 1, teams with the same record will be placed in a common pool. Matchups will be
      drawn from those pools for each round. The only rule for Round 2 and 3 of the Group
      Stage is there will be no rematches directly from Masters Toronto. As a result, regional
      matchups are permitted in Rounds 2 and 3.

Round 2 - Draw
Round 2 of the Group Stage Draw will include all eight teams in the Group Stage. This
will be the second of three (3) rounds for the Masters Toronto Group Stage. Teams who
won their first match in Round 1 will be in a common pool. Teams who lost their first
match in Round 1 will be in a separate, common pool.

Teams from the same region are eligible to face each other in Rounds 2 and 3. Teams are
placed into the bracket using a random draw.

Pool Assignments: 
      Pool 1 - Team 1-0, Team 1-0, Team 1-0, Team 1-0
      Pool 2 - Team 0-1, Team 0-1, Team 0-1, Team 0-1

Round 2 - Seeding

As teams are drawn, the first team drawn into each Match for Round 2 and 3 of Group
Stage will obtain the higher seed and get to choose Team A or Team B in map selection.

Round 3 - Draw
Round 3 of the Group Stage Draw will include four teams with a record of 1-1 through
Masters Toronto Group Stage. This will be the final of three (3) rounds for the Masters
Toronto Group Stage. All teams will be in the same pool.

Teams from the same region are eligible to face each other in Rounds 2 and 3. Rematches
from within the Masters Toronto Group Stage are not permitted. If a team is drawn into a
matchup that has already been played at Masters Toronto, they will be placed in the next
available spot. If they were drawn into the last slot (i.e. Match 2, Slot 2), they will swap
with the team immediately before them until a valid draw is complete.

Pool Assignments: 
      Pool 1 - Team 1-1, Team 1-1, Team 1-1, Team 1-1

Round 3 - Seeding

As teams are drawn, the first team drawn into each Match for Round 2 and 3 of Group
Stage will obtain the higher seed and get to choose Team A or Team B in map selection.

Playoffs - Draw

      The Playoffs Draw will include the top four (4) teams emerging from Group Stage with a
      record of either 2-0 or 2-1, and the four (4) #1 seed Teams from the respective VCT
      Leagues.

      Each #1 seeded Team will send a representative of their choice to the stage. The four #1
      seed Teams will be randomly drawn. The drawn #1 seed Team will then CHOOSE their
      opponent from the pool of teams who progressed out of the Group Stage (Pool 2).
      Another #1 seed Team cannot be selected. However, regional selections are legal.


      Pool Assignments: 
            Pool 1 - #1 Seed Teams           
            Pool 2 - Team 2-0, Team 2-0, Team 2-1, Team 2-1




      Playoffs - Seeding

      Teams from Pool 1 will be the highest seeds, followed by teams from Pool 2. If teams
      from the same Pool meet in Upper Round 2, Lower Round 1, or Upper Finals there will
      be a coin flip to decide the higher seed for map pick/ban selection. For Lower Round 2,
      Lower Semifinals, Lower Finals and Grand Finals, the team coming from the Upper
      bracket will automatically be the higher seed, regardless of Pool.

3.   Roster Rules
      3.1. Starting Rosters
      For each match of the International Competition, the Team must designate five (5)
      starters from the Team’s Active Roster (as defined in the VCT25 Roster Construction
      Rules), which shall constitute the Team’s “Starting Roster”. Rosters are considered
      public at the submission deadline.

<!-- página 6 -->
         Requests to modify a Starting Roster for a Team’s first match on any day must be
         submitted no later than 10:00pm local time, or an hour after the conclusion of the final
         game of the day, whichever is the latest, the day prior to the team’s match.

         3.2. Substitutions during Tournament Play
         For Matches involving more than one map (i.e., a best-of-three or best-of-five Match), a
         Team may replace their then-current starters with substitutes from the Team’s Active
         Roster (as defined in the VCT25 Roster Construction Rules) in between maps, provided
         that the Team informs the opposing Team and receives approval from the referees of such
         substitution no later than two (2) minutes after the conclusion of the previous game. Any
         substitutions must result in the Team having an eligible roster.
         Once a Team decides to use a substitution, the substitute player must immediately enter
         the Match Area and will be given seven (7) minutes to complete the setup process.


4.      Accommodations and Player Practice Rooms
         All twelve Teams will be provided with accommodations and player practice rooms for
         the required five (5) starters, up to one (1) substitute, one (1) Head Coach, and one (1)
         team manager who have earned the right to participate in International Competitions held
         as part of the VCT.
         In order to maintain a fair and equal competition, and facilitate the smooth running of the
         event, all Teams must use the accommodations, player practice rooms, and other
         competition facilities provided by the League. Unless approved at the sole discretion of
         the League, Teams are prohibited from using other accommodations or player practice
         rooms during the course of the event.

5.      Competitive Patch
         All matches will be played on patch version 10.10.

6.      Map Pool
         The map pool consists of Ascent, Haven, Icebox, Lotus, Pearl, Split, and Sunset.

8.   Travel and Expenses
      For the required five (5) starters, up to one (1) substitute, one (1) Head Coach, and one
      (1) team manager per Team who have earned the right to participate in International
      Competitions held as part of the VCT, the League Entity will provide reasonable travel,
      accommodation and meals while they compete at such events. Reimbursement for
      reasonable travel, accommodation and meals while they compete at such events must be
      approved by League Entity. Players who qualify to compete in any International
      Competitions held as part of the VCT must have all necessary visas, passports or other
      travel documents for travel to the city where the events are held, and agree to comply
      with any health and safety requirements established by the League Entity and/or
      Applicable Law. Players under the age of majority under applicable law may be required
      to travel with a parent or legal guardian. In such cases, the League Entity may also
      provide a single parent or guardian for each player under the age of majority with
      reasonable travel, accommodation and meals or reimbursement for the foregoing while
      such player competes at the International Competition held as part of the VCT.

9.   Priority and Conflicts.
      In the event of a conflict between (a) any term of this Event-Specific Ruleset, the
      Competition Ruleset, and the terms of a Team Participation Agreement, or (b) any term
      of this Event-Specific Ruleset and the terms of a Guidance Document, the League Entity,
      in its sole discretion, shall determine the terms that shall govern and prevail. Any
      remedies set forth in this Event-Specific Ruleset or a Guidance Document shall be in
      addition to, and shall not supersede, any remedies set forth a Team Participation
      Agreement.
