1.   Introduction and Purpose
      1.1. Background.
      VALORANT Champions Tour LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (“League
      Entity”), along with its affiliates (collectively, “Riot”), organizes a series of regional,
      cross-regional, and international esports tournaments and related media events under the
      name VALORANT Champions Tour (the “VCT”) which feature competitive play (both
      live and online) of the video game VALORANT (the “Game”) by professional esports
      players and teams. The following are each a “VCT League” or “League”: (i) VCT
      Americas, (ii) VCT Pacific, and (iii) VCT EMEA. The League Entity has entered into
      written agreements with certain team-owning entities (each, a “Team Entity”) governing
      right to compete in the VCT and their respective Leagues (each such agreement, a “Team
      Participation Agreement”). Pursuant to the Team Participation Agreements and as more
      fully described therein, the League Entity has granted each Team Entity the right to field
      a team that will compete in the VCT and its respective League (“Team”).

      1.2. Purpose.
      The League Entity has created this VCT Pacific Kickoff Event-Specific Ruleset (the
      “Event-Specific Ruleset”) to set forth certain policies, rules, and procedures that will
      apply to all Teams participating in the VCT Pacific Kickoff event. This Event-Specific
      Ruleset is in addition to, and not in lieu of, the VALORANT Official Competition Ruleset
      (the “Competition Ruleset”) and the VCT26 Roster Construction Rules (the “RCR”).

2.   VCT Pacific Kickoff Structure

      2.1. Kickoff - Draw Procedure

      2.1.1. Teams will be split into two pools based on performance in the 2025 season, with
              the four (4) VCT Champions teams in Pool 1, and the remaining eight (8) teams in
              Pool 2. Teams in Pool 1 will receive a bye in the opening round.

2.2. Kickoff - Competitive Format

2.2.1. Teams will play in a triple elimination bracket. The four teams that qualified to
       VCT Champions in the 2025 season will receive a first round bye, with the
       remaining eight teams facing off in the opening round.



2.2.2. All Matches will be best-of-three (“Bo3”), except the Upper Bracket Final,
        Middle Bracket Final and Lower Bracket Final which will be best-of-five
        (“Bo5”).



2.2.3. Seeding for map selection purposes will be as follows:
        ● Matches 5/6/7/8 - teams receiving a bye are considered the higher seeds.
        ● Matches 9/10/11/12 - the losing teams from Matches 5/6/7/8 are considered
           the higher seeds.
        ● All other Middle and Lower bracket matches, where one team is coming from
           the upper bracket, the team dropping from the upper bracket will be
           considered the higher seed.
        ● In all other matches, a Skirmish match will be used to determine the higher
           seed.



2.2.4. The top three (3) teams from VCT KICK//OFF will qualify for Masters Santiago.

2.3.2. Championship Point Tiebreakers

<!-- página 7 -->
   ● Championship point tie-breakers will primarily consider each Team’s full-season 
       performance in their respective region’s VCT League, including results from
       International Competitions. The following tie-breaker process will be used to
       determine which Team holds the higher position in championship points.

2.3.3. Championship Point Tie-Breaker Process

       Note: Criteria 9–11 do not apply to ties involving three or more Teams.

       (1) Final standings from Stage 2
       (2) Final standings from Masters 2
       (3) Final standings from Stage 1
       (4) Final standings from Masters 1
       (5) Final standings from KICK//OFF
       (6) Total year/season Match Wins (excluding Global Events, Play-In, Playoffs and
       KICK//OFF)
       (7) Total year/season Map Diff (excluding Global Events, Play-In, Playoffs and
       KICK//OFF)
       (8) Total year/season Round Diff (excluding Global Events, Play-In, Playoffs and
       KICK//OFF)
       (9) Head-to-head Match score
       (10) Head-to-head map differential
       (11) Head-to-head round differential


       ● Head-to-head match score, map differential, and round differential are
          weighted equally, regardless of match stage (including Playoffs, Play-In) or
          format (Bo3/Bo5). If no head-to-head data exists, the next tiebreaker criteria
          apply.

       ● Playoff/Play-In performance and qualification takes precedence in VCT
          League final standings. Among non-playoff/play-in Teams (including teams
          that have the same standing from the Playoffs/Play-In):
          
          (a) If all non-playoff/play-in Teams are in the same group, group standings
          apply (with tiebreakers if needed).
          
          (b) Otherwise, the non-playoff/play-in Teams are considered tied, and further
          criteria apply.

       ● The following “Tie-breaker General Rules” will be applied for 3+ Team
          tie-breakers:

<!-- página 8 -->
           ○ If any Team(s) can be clearly ranked based on a single applicable
              tiebreaker criterion (e.g., head-to-head match score), those Team(s) will
              confirm their standing and be removed from the tie.

           ○ For any remaining Teams that are still tied under the same criterion, the
              tiebreaker process will be reapplied from the beginning among the tied
              Teams.

           ○ Example: In a 4-way tie, if Team A (3–0) and Team D (0–3) can be
              clearly ranked as first and fourth respectively while Team B and Team
              C remain tied (1–1), Team A and Team D confirm their standings and
              are removed from the tie. The tiebreaker process is reapplied from the
              beginning between Team B and Team C to determine their final
              placements.

           ○ In the event that multiple ties occur simultaneously, each tie shall be
              resolved independently within its respective subgroup. For each
              subgroup, the tiebreaker process will be reapplied from the beginning,
              using the standard tiebreaker procedures outlined in the ‘Tie-Breaker
              Process’ above. The final placement of teams will preserve subgroup
              hierarchy: the highest-ranked team within a lower-ranked subgroup
              may not be placed above any team in a higher-ranked subgroup,
              regardless of subsequent tiebreaker outcomes within the subgroup.

           ○ This process will continue until all teams are fully ranked.

      ● If a tie still remains after applying all procedures, a Bo1 match may be
         implemented at the discretion of League Officials. An alternative process
         may be implemented on a case-by-case basis.



2.4. Skirmish Rulesets


      ● In matches where teams are of equal seeding, a 1v1 Skirmish match will be
         played to determine the higher seeded team for map selection purposes. At
         their sole discretion, League Officials may choose to utilise Skirmish
         matches to determine seeding in any other official match.

      ● Each team will be required to submit a participating player(s) for the
         Skirmish alongside starting rosters prior to each match. This player must be
         part of the starting roster for the match.

<!-- página 9 -->
      ● Skirmishes will be played from a designated location and at a specified time
         communicated by League officials.

      ● League officials will outline areas that content teams and other team
         personnel may be present during the execution of the Skirmish Match.

      ● Participating players will be given a small time window to set up gear and
         check settings. League officials will instruct players on time allocated and
         when the skirmish is starting. These areas may differ in configuration to
         “Match Areas” outlined in the VCT Ruleset.

      ● Map layout selection will be made at random by League officials.

      ● Winners of the skirmish are defined as the first participant to reach a score of
         5 rounds.

      ● League officials have the right at any point to impose gameplay restrictions
         including but not limited to the use of certain agents, weapons, tactics.
         Ability usage is strictly prohibited in any case they are enabled during the
         skirmish.

      ● League officials have the right at any point to assign victory based on new
         point win thresholds communicated to teams prior to match day.

      ● League officials have the right to utilize a coinflip in the event of a technical
         or network failure that prevents the skirmish from being played.

      ● Failure to arrive by specified timings for skirmishes outlined by league
         officials may result in penalties including but not limited to fines and
         forfeiture of draft choice.

3.   Roster Rules
      3.1. Starting Rosters
      For each match of VCT Pacific Kickoff, the Team must designate ﬁve (5) starters from
      the Team’s Active Roster (as defined in the VCT26 Roster Construction Rules), which
      shall constitute the Team’s “Starting Roster”. Rosters are considered public at the
      submission deadline.
      Requests to modify a Starting Roster for a Team’s first match on any day must be
      submitted no later than 10:00pm local time, or an hour after the conclusion of the final
      game of the day, whichever is the latest, the day prior to the team’s match.


      3.2. Substitutions during Tournament Play
      For Matches involving more than one map (i.e., a best-of-three or best-of-five Match), a
      Team may replace their then-current starters with substitutes from the Team’s Active
      Roster (as defined in the VCT26 Roster Construction Rules) in between maps, provided
      that the Team informs the opposing Team and receives approval from the referees of such
      substitution no later than two (2) minutes after the conclusion of the previous game. In
      the event that a player disconnects during a map and is unable to return within the
      allocated pause time, the Team will be permitted to replace the player with a substitute
      from their Active Roster. Any substitutions must result in the Team having an eligible
      roster.
      Once a Team decides to use a substitution, the substitute player must immediately enter
      the Match Area and will be given seven (7) minutes to complete the setup process.

6.   Precautionary Notes

      6.1. Player Negligence
      Any pauses or delays (broadcast or operations) due to player negligence may be subject
      to penalties and may not be granted a tech pause. Player negligence includes, but is not
      limited to, activities such as alt-tabbing or pressing keys that black out their monitors,
      refusal/hesitancy to use venue-provided peripherals when players have not brought their
      own backup peripherals in cases of technical malfunctions, frequent hand warmer bursts,
      etc. League Officials will have full discretion to determine instances of player negligence.


      6.2. Venue entrance for non-staff members
      Non-staff members must obtain written authorization from League Officials at least seven
      (7) business days prior to entering Back of House (BOH) areas. In the absence of such
      written authorization, non-staff members are strictly prohibited from entering BOH areas.
      League Officials retain sole discretion to approve or deny requests for access. Any
      unauthorized entry by non-staff members may result in penalties for the associated
      players or teams.

6.3. Indoor Smoking Regulations
Indoor smoking is strictly prohibited at the venue, particularly due to the venue being a
government-owned property. Any violation of this rule may jeopardize the entire event.
As such, smoking indoors is absolutely prohibited.


6.4. Onstage Huddle Limits before Match 2
Following the on-stage walkout and prior to the start of Match 2, Teams are permitted to
engage in a Team huddle. However, this huddle must not exceed one (1) minute in
duration.
If a huddle exceeds the allotted time, referees or on-stage staff may instruct the team to
immediately take their seats and prepare for the Match.
Repeated or egregious delays may result in penalties, at the discretion of League
Officials. Teams are expected to promptly comply with all instructions to avoid delaying
the match schedule.


6.5   Application of the Code of Conduct at the Venue
The Riot Games Esports Global Code of Conduct applies at all times, including when
Esports Professionals are not on broadcast or in areas that are typically shown on
broadcast.
This includes private team communications using Riot provided peripherals (e.g.,
between coaches over comms) and conduct in areas such as huddle rooms or coach
rooms. Some of these areas may be monitored by referees or recorded by broadcast
equipment, and any unprofessional behavior or language may be subject to review and
penalties.


6.6  Non-Native and True-Stretched Resolution Ban
Starting with Kickoff for the 2026 season we are updating our policy to ban the use of
non-native and true-stretched resolutions to ensure fairness, stability, and a better
experience for players and fans.
   ● Competitive Integrity: Non-native resolutions alter the intended game state and
      can create unintended visibility differences. Standardizing resolutions helps
      maintain a fair playing field.

<!-- página 13 -->
         ● Performance & Supportability: These resolutions operate outside what we can
            reliably support and have been linked to stability issues, making troubleshooting
            difficult for both players and event staff.

         ● Broadcast Quality: True-stretched POVs distort replays and spectator footage,
            impacting the viewing experience.

         ● Operational & Security Concerns: Achieving true-stretched requires manual,
            multi-step workarounds that slow event operations, creating security risks and
            delays during setup and maintenance.
     Players playing with non-native and true-stretched resolutions may be penalized or asked 
     to readjust their settings to approved resolution forms.
