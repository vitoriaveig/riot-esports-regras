1.​   Introduction and Purpose

      1.1.​   Background

      Riot Games Services Pte. Ltd. (“League Entity”), along with its affiliates (collectively,
      “Riot”), organizes a series of cross-regional esports tournaments and related media
      events under the name League of Legends Championship Pacific (the “LCP”) which
      feature competitive play (both live and online) of the video game League of Legends
      (the “League of Legends”) by professional esports players and teams. The League
      Entity has entered into written agreements with certain team-owning entities (each, a
      “Team Entity”) governing the right to compete in the LCP. Pursuant to the Team
      Participation Agreements (“TPA”) and as more fully described therein, the League
      Entity has granted each Team Entity the right to field a team that will compete in the
      LCP (“Team”).

      1.2.​   Purpose

      The League Entity has created this LCP Official Competition Ruleset (the
      “Competition Ruleset”) to set forth certain policies, rules and procedures that will
      apply to all Team Entities that have entered into Team Participation Agreements with
      the League Entity and been admitted by contract to compete in the LCP. The
      Competition Ruleset forms part of the broader set of rules and policies described in
      those agreements (“Rules”). The Competition Ruleset is intended for a broader
      audience that includes the public and the press. The League Entity may issue
      additional event-specific policies, rules and procedures that will apply to all Team
      Entities participating in the applicable event (“Event-Specific Ruleset”). These
      Event-Specific Rulesets are in addition to, and not in lieu of, the Competition Ruleset.

      1.3.​   Acknowledgement and Acceptance

      The acknowledgement of the Competition Ruleset, and an agreement to be bound by
      and comply with their terms, is an essential term of each TPA. This Competition
      Ruleset is the “Official Competition Ruleset” referenced in each Team Participation
      Agreement. The League Entity may issue interpretations, guidance and other policy
      documents (each, a “Guidance Document”) from time to time in order to explain,
      clarify, supplement or provide informal guidance on the provisions of the Policies, the
      Competition Ruleset and the other Rules. All such Guidance Documents shall be
      deemed to be incorporated into, and form part of, the Competition Ruleset upon their
      release to the Teams.

      1.4.​   Application

      Each Team Entity acknowledges and agrees that a breach of, or failure to comply
      with, the Competition Ruleset or any Guidance Document by (a) any person or entity
      who, directly or indirectly through any intermediate corporations or other entities,
      owns securities, equity interests or other ownership interests in the Team Entity or
      the Team Marks (“Owner”) shall be deemed a breach of, or failure to comply with, the
      Competition Ruleset or any Guidance Document by the Team Entity; and (b) any

The League Entity shall be entitled to undertake such disciplinary procedures as it
determines in relation to any breach of, or failure to comply with, this Competition
Ruleset by the Team Entity, any Owners or Team Personnel and to impose such
fines, suspensions, disqualifications and other disciplinary actions (or combinations
thereof) as the League Entity determines in its sole discretion (collectively,
“Disciplinary Actions”); and such Disciplinary Actions (i) may be publicly disclosed
by the League Entity as determined in its sole discretion, (ii) are reasonable and
necessary in order to maintain the competitive integrity of the LCP or the goodwill
associated with League of Legends, the LCP, and (iii) are dealt with in compliance
with the Riot Games Esports Global Code of Conduct (“Code of Conduct”), as well
as with the TPA and all relevant Rules.

1.6.​   Due Diligence Requirements

Teams shall fulfill all due diligence requirements prior to their participation in the LCP.
Failure by the Teams to meet these requirements by the specified deadlines set by
the League Entity may result in disqualification or other penalties as determined by
the League Entity.

1.7.​   Defined Terms

Capitalized terms in this Competition Ruleset, unless expressly defined otherwise
herein, shall have the meaning ascribed to such terms in the applicable TPA.

1.8.​   Official Language

English is the official language of the LCP. All official communications, documents,
and disciplinary proceedings shall be conducted in English. It is the responsibility of
Team Personnel to ensure that all relevant parties fully comprehend these
communications. In the event of any conflict in interpretation between the English
version and a translation of this Competition Ruleset or other official documents, the
English version will govern and control, unless otherwise provided by applicable law.

2.​   Tournament Venue, Competition Area Layout, and Schedule

      2.1.​     General Venue Access

      Access to restricted areas of venues for official Matches is strictly limited to Team
      Personnel, unless otherwise approved in advance by the League Officials. Access to
      the venue, including restricted areas, are subject to the sole discretion of League
      Officials.

      2.2.​     Pre-Entry Health Inspection

      Prior to entering the venue for official Matches, each Team Personnel must verify
      their identity with League Officials. Additionally, League Officials reserves the right to
      conduct health inspections, which may include temperature checks or other
      measures deemed necessary to confirm that individuals are physically fit to enter the
      venue.

      If, at any time before or during a Match, League Officials determine that an
      individual’s health status is unsuitable for entry, the individual will be denied entry and
      will be required to leave the venue immediately. Should a Player be deemed unfit to
      participate in a Match, the League Officials may require the team to provide a
      substitute player. If applicable law mandates any additional or alternative health
      inspection, sanitation or public safety protocols, League Officials reserves the right to
      implement such procedures, and all Team Personnel and Players are required to
      comply with these measures.

      2.3.​     Tournament Venue & Competition Layout

      The competition layout within the Tournament Venue is subject to change based on
      the size and specific conditions of the venue. If a Team requires additional access for
      any other individuals beyond the standard Team Personnel due to extenuating
      circumstances, prior approval must be obtained from League Officials. The League
      Officials reserve the right, at their discretion, change or limit the number of persons
      granted access to the Tournament Venue.

              2.3.1.​   Team Personnel

                Only credentialed Team Personnel, including Players, Coaches, General
                Managers, Team Managers, Translators, and other Team support staff that
                have been registered and issued credentials for the event will be allowed to
                enter the Tournament Venue.

              2.3.2.​   Tournament Venue

                The Tournament Venue is the designated physical area where the tournament
                takes place. This includes, but is not limited to, the Match Area, player
                preparation zones, audience seating, interview areas, administrative offices,

        2.3.3.​   Match Area

          The “Match Area” encompasses the immediate area surrounding any
          competition PCs used during Match at the Tournament Venue, including the
          stage where the Players or Teams compete, as well as any spaces
          designated for referees, technical staff, camera, and equipment.

          During the Match, only the participating Players from each Team are
          permitted in the Match Area.

        2.3.4.​   Waiting Room/Pre-match Room

          The “Waiting Room” or “Pre-match Room” is the designated area for Teams to
          prepare, conduct warm-up activities and await the start of a Match.

        2.3.5.​   Huddle Room/Green Room

          The “Huddle Room” or “Green Room” is the designated area for Team
          Personnel to remain during a Match. This space may contain spectator PCs
          and will be designated by the League Officials.

        2.3.6.​   Interview Areas

          The interview areas, including Back-stage Interview (BSI) & Press Interview
          zones, are designated space within the Tournament Venue for conducting
          interviews with players, teams, or other Team Personnel.

2.4.​     Tournament Venue Assets Responsibility

All Team Personnel are responsible for ensuring the proper use and care of assets
provided within the Tournament Venue, including but not limited to equipment, PCs
and chairs, supplied peripherals, credentials, and other facilities. Team Personnel
must exercise diligence to ensure these assets are not damaged, broken, or
otherwise compromised. In the event of any damage, misuse, or compromise of
assets by a Player or other Team Personnel, League Officials reserve the right to
impose penalties. Such penalties may include fines to cover repair or replacement
costs, additional sanctions, or both, at the discretion of League Officials.

3.​   Equipments, Peripherals & On-stage Devices

      3.1.​     Player or Team-owned Equipment

              3.1.1.​   Permitted Player-owned or Team-owned Equipment.

                At LCP events, Players may bring the following Player or Team owned
                equipment into the Match Area for use:

                           1.​ PC keyboards
                           2.​ PC mice and cord holder
                           3.​ mouse pads

                Players must use headsets and microphones provided by League Officials.
                The League Entity will not be responsible for the functionality of the Player or
                Team-owned equipment. If such equipment is found to be incompatible with
                League Officials-provided equipment, League Officials will provide
                replacement equipment(s), which must be used for the remainder of the
                Match without exception.

              3.1.2.​   Required Approvals for Use of Team-Owned Equipment.

                All Team-owned Equipment used during LCP events may be subject to
                competitive integrity checks by League Officials. These checks may occur
                during designated testing periods prior to entering the Match Area for set-up.
                If requested, the Team must submit their Team-owned Equipment for
                inspection to League Officials before the start of the Match.

                Any unapproved equipment or equipment suspected of providing an unfair
                competitive advantage will not be permitted for use in the Match. In such
                cases, Players must use League Officials-provided equipment. Additionally,
                League Officials, at their discretion, may disallow the use of any equipment
                for reasons related to tournament security, safety or operational efficiency. No
                Team-owned Equipment featuring or displaying the name, likeness or logo of
                a company or brand unapproved by the League Officials is allowed in the
                Match Area. League Officials reserve the right to request Team to cover-up a
                logo or brand on Team-owned Equipment during the event.

              3.1.3.​   Replacement Team-owned Equipment

                If a technical issue related or suspected to equipment happens, a Player or
                League Officials may request a technical review. League Officials or a
                technician designated by League Officials will diagnose and troubleshoot the
                suspected issues. If necessary, League Officials may authorize equipment
                replacement. If a Player wishes to use player/team owned equipment as a
                replacement, it must be available on-site and approved by League Officials. If
                unavailable, the League-provided equipment will be used for the remainder of
                the Match. Decisions regarding the replacement of any equipment are at the
                sole discretion of the League Officials.

3.2.​     League Equipment

League Officials will provide, and Players will exclusively use, the following
equipment for all LCP events:

              ●​ PC and monitor
              ●​ headsets and microphones
              ●​ tables and chairs

Upon request, League Officials will provide the following:

              ●​   PC keyboards
              ●​   PC mice
              ●​   mouse pads
              ●​   water bottles/tumblers

All League-provided equipment will be selected at the sole discretion of the League
Officials. Unauthorized modification or the use of unapproved hardware, software, or
equipment may be deemed to be a breach of the Rules. Players are required to
return all League-provided equipment upon the conclusion of the event or upon
request.

3.3.​     Computer Programs and Usage

Players and Coaches are prohibited from installing or using personal programs on
League-provided equipment, which includes those in the Waiting Room and Huddle
Room. Approval from League Officials is required before installing any program.

        3.3.1.​    Voice Chat

          Voice chat will be provided exclusively through League-provided programs.
          Use of third-party voice chat software (e.g., Discord) is prohibited without prior
          approval from League Officials. League Officials will monitor a Team’s audio
          and voice chat during their participation in the Huddle Room and Match Area.

        3.3.2.​    Social Media and Communication

          Team Personnel are prohibited from accessing or posting on social media or
          communication platforms (e.g, Facebook, X (formerly Twitter), forums, emails)
          using League-provided equipment.

        3.3.3.​    Non-Essential Equipment

          No devices or peripherals, other than permitted Team-owned equipment, or
          League-provided equipment, may be connected to League-provided
          computers, for any reason. This includes, but is not limited to, mobile phones,
          unauthorized equipment, and flash drives.

        3.3.4.​   Macro Programs

          All macro programs, including software macros and hardware implemented
          macros stored in on-board memory of the hardware, external to the League of
          Legends client are strictly prohibited.

        3.3.5.​   Native Programs

          Players may use the native programs, such as mouse controllers, monitor
          adjustment, only before and after Matches, not during. League Officials may
          restrict access to these native programs in case of misuse.

        3.3.6.​   Audio Restrictions

          Unless otherwise requested by Players and approved by League Officials,
          Players must maintain a minimum volume level, as determined by League
          Officials. Headphones must be worn directly on a Player’s ears for the whole
          duration of the Match.

          No items, except for certain religious articles and/or medical devices (e.g.,
          hijab, dastar, yarmulke and hearing devices) may be placed between the
          headphones and a Player’s ears. League Officials will determine whether an
          item qualifies for exemption.

        3.3.7.​   Equipment Handling

          Players and Coaches are prohibited from touching or handling other player’s
          equipment before or during a Match. Players may assist in collecting their
          Team Member’s peripherals only after the Match has concluded. For
          equipment assistance, Players may request help from the League Officials.

3.4.​     Wireless Devices

No wireless electronic devices, including, but not limited to: mobile phones, tablets,
health devices and smart watches are permitted in the Match Area during the Match
(which for the avoidance of doubt, includes the ban/pick phase, pauses, remakes,
and between games of multi-game Matches), unless pre-approved by League
Officials. League Officials will collect such devices from all Team Members before
entering the Match Area and will return them after the end of the Match.

Players requiring health-related support devices must submit the device model and
relevant health information to League Officials at least forty-eight (48) hours before
the Match start time for approval.

3.5.​     Notes and Notepads

Players are prohibited from bringing any written or printed materials into the Match
Area during a Match. Coaches and Analysts are permitted to bring written or printed
materials into the Match Area during a Match, but must keep them in their designated

        3.7.1.​   Dress code

5.2.​     First Selection

The Right of First Selection (“RoFS”) refers to a selection advantage between two
pre-game choices: preferred pick position (First Pick / Second Pick) or preferred map
side (Blue / Red).

Both Teams will submit their selection for both choices in preferred order to the
League Officials:
   ●​ Preferred order is the order of RoFS submitted, followed by the most and 2nd
      preferred choice (i.e. “First Pick, Red Side”).
   ●​ The Team with RoFS will be granted their most preferred choice (i.e. “First
      Pick”).
   ●​ The Team without RoFS will be granted their preference on the choice that
      the initial team did not select (i.e. “Red Side”).

In any BO3 or BO5 matches, the losing Team of the previous game will earn the
RoFS for the next game. Both Teams must submit their selection within 5 minutes
from the Nexus explosion.

The selection that is not used will be kept confidential and not shared with the
opposing Team. If the Team with the RoFS fails to submit their selection by the said
time, they will default to the “First Pick”. Their opponent will be granted their
preference on the choice that the initial team did not select. In the situation when the
opponent team does not submit their choice, they will default to the “Blue Side” or
“First Pick”, whatever was not initially selected. League Officials will announce the
selection immediately after the deadline.

        5.2.1.​   Submission Timeline

          For the Regular Season phase set forth in the Event-Specific Ruleset, Teams
          must submit their first selection no later than 9:00 pm GMT+8 or an hour after
          the conclusion of the final game of the day, whichever is the latest, a day prior

          For KnockOut and PlayOff phase, Team will submit their selection to the
          League Officials at a predesignated time which will be announced at least
          seven (7) days before the Match day.

          opponent team does not submit their choice, they will default to the “Blue
          Side”. League Officials will announce the selection immediately after the

7.​   Interruption of Gameplay & Match Process
      7.1.​     Definition of Terms & Process

              7.1.1.​   Unintentional Disconnection

                An “unintentional disconnect” refers to the situation when a Player
                unintentionally loses connection to the game due to problems or issues with
                the LoL client, platform, network, or PC. If a disconnection or any other failure
                occurs which interrupts the loading process and prevents a Player from
                joining the game, the game must be immediately paused. League Officials, at
                their sole discretion, will provide further instructions on the match process as
                outlined in this section.

              7.1.2.​   Intentional Disconnection

                Intentional Disconnections are not allowed. Players may experience an
                intentional disconnection when their actions (e.g. quitting the game) cause
                them to lose connection to the game. Any actions of a player which lead to a
                disconnection shall be deemed intentional, regardless of the actual intent of
                the player. In the case of an Intentional Disconnection, League officials are
                not required to enforce stoppages.

              7.1.3.​   Game of Record

                A game of record (“GOR”) refers to a game where all ten (10) Players have
                loaded in and meaningful interaction between opposing teams have occurred.
                Once a game achieves GOR status, incidental restarts will no longer be
                permitted, and the game will be considered “official”. After GOR is
                established, game remakes will only be allowed only under limited conditions.
                GOR is typically established under the following conditions:

                    ●​ Any attack or ability is landed on minions, jungle creeps, structures or
                       enemy Champions;
                    ●​ Establishing line-of-sight between Players of opposing Teams;
                    ●​ Setting foot, establishing vision or targeting skillshot ability in the
                       opponent’s jungle by either Team, which includes either leaving the
                       river or entering brush connected to enemy jungle;

              ●​ Game timer reaches two minutes (00:02:00).

        7.1.4.​   Prompt Reporting

          Upon identifying a bug, including hardware failures, the affected Player must
          pause the game as soon as practically possible using one of the following
          methods and alert the Referee (“Prompt Report”):

              ●​ Use the /pause command;
              ●​ Request a teammate to pause via voice communications;
              ●​ Request the Referee to pause the game.

          If a Player verbally requests that the Referee pause the game, even if the
          game is not immediately paused, the Player will be considered to have made
          a Prompt Report. If circumstances make it impractical to pause immediately,
          such as an active engagement between two Teams, the Referee may still
          consider the report as a Prompt Report if they determine it was impractical to
          pause the game until the engagement ended.

          However, if there is a significant delay between the bug occurrence and its
          discovery or the bug is reported after the game ends, it will not be considered
          to be a Prompt Report, even if the above conditions and methods are
          satisfied. A restart, Chronobreak, or remake may not be offered unless a
          Prompt Report occurs.

7.3.​     Game Bug

A bug refers to an error, flaw, failure or fault that produces an incorrect or unexpected

          The League Officials will provide a list of predefined bugs for competitive play
          in each patch, that indicates the known bug which could happen during a
          game. If no updated list is provided, the previous patch’s list will remain in
          effect.

        7.3.2.​   Minor Bug

          A minor bug is defined as an inconvenience that does not significantly hinder
          gameplay. Examples include bugs that alter game stats or gameplay
          mechanics in a manner that, while not optimal, can be played through if
          necessary.

        7.3.3.​   Critical Bug

          A critical bug significantly affects a Player’s ability to compete or significantly
          alters game stats or gameplay mechanics, or the gameplay environment (i.e.,
          the game User Interface (UI) has an error) becomes untenable. League
          Officials will determine, at their discretion, if the bug meets the criteria of a
          critical bug.

7.5.​     Game Chronobreak

Chronobreak is a tool used to roll back the competitive game to a previous state
before the current gameplay is taking place.


                                                                                         34

<!-- página 35 -->
If a bug occurs in the game at any point during a match, the League Officials will first
determine whether the Player followed the pause protocol (e.g., Prompt Reporting). If
the game has been paused in due process, League Officials will determine whether
the bug is a verifiable bug. If it is a verifiable bug, the League Officials will next
determine whether the bug requires Chronobreak or not.

     7.5.1.​   “Dead-Ball“ State

       A “Dead-Ball” state refers to a point in a game when neither Team is heavily
       engaged with each other.

       When executing Chronobreak, if the bug occurred during no or minor
       engagement, the Dead-Ball state should be as close as possible to the bug
       instance.

       When executing Chronobreak, if the bug occurred during heavy engagement,
       the Dead-Ball state should be set at least two (2) seconds before the
       engagement began.

       A perfect Dead-Ball state may not always exist. In such cases, the priority is
       to identify a point as close to the bug as possible without major engagements
       at League Official sole decision. Rolling back too far might undo legitimate
       gameplay actions (e.g., ward placement, lane pressure, flanking).

     7.5.2.​   Condition for Chronobreak

       If the bug is a critical bug (not a play through bug), the League Officials will
       determine (i) whether Chronobreak can restore the game; and (ii) whether
       reverting the game to an earlier state will fix the bug or avoid the trigger
       condition for the bug.

       If Chronobreak can restore the game to an ideal state that would help resolve
       the bug, then the Chronobreak process will be initiated.

       If Chronobreak cannot restore the game, or reverting the game to an earlier
       state will not solve the issue, the bug will result in a remake.

     7.5.3.​   Chronobreak Process

       If the bug is a critical bug, League Officials will determine if the bug
       significantly disadvantaged one or both Teams. If so, the disadvantaged
       Team(s) will be offered Chronobreak. The Players of such Team(s) must
       decide within the time designated by League Officials whether to use
       Chronobreak or not. Failure to decide in time will be considered a decision not
       to use Chronobreak.

       If both Teams were significantly disadvantaged, Chronobreak will be
       implemented at the request of either Team. However, the Teams will not be
       told the specific Dead-Ball state before making their decision.

          Officials will set the Dead-Ball state to a point prior to the bug and inform both

        7.5.4.​   Unsuccessful Chronobreak

7.7.​     Hardware Malfunction

Hardware malfunction refers to any unintentional failure of any piece of hardware,
including server failure, monitor or PC failure or player peripheral failure.
Player-induced failures (e.g., intentional damage) are not considered hardware
malfunctions. League Officials will determine whether the malfunction was intentional
or unintentional.

League Officials will also determine whether the hardware malfunction constitutes a
minor bug (e.g., a monitor loses power temporarily while the Player is not engaged
with the opposite Team), a critical bug (e.g., a keyboard stops working, causing a
Player death) or a terminal situation (e.g., the game server crashes), and follow the
appropriate standard above.

will ask both Teams to confirm readiness to resume play through in-game chat. Once
both Teams confirm readiness, League Officials will resume the game.

The Esports Penalty Index does not preclude Riot's discretion to make case-by-case

discretionary right to impose further disciplinary measures, as deemed necessary.

breach of the Riot Games Esports Global Code of Conduct ("Code").

Any violation of the Code and corresponding competition rulesets will be thoroughly
reviewed on a case-by-case basis, with penalties potentially including permanent bans and
substantial fines. Failure to cooperate fully with investigations may result in penalties
commensurate with those for the breaches under investigation.
