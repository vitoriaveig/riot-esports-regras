1. Introduction
      1.1.   Background
              Riot Games, Inc. (“RGI”) and/or its affiliate companies (collectively referred to as
              “Riot,” “Riot Entities,” or individually as a “Riot Entity”) have established the
              North American Challengers League Rulebook (“Rules”) for competitive play of
              League of Legends (“LoL”) in the North American region. These Rules are
              designed solely to ensure the integrity of the North American Challengers League
              (“NACL”), with “League Entity” referring to the applicable Riot Entity in each
              contract.
      1.2.   Application of the Rules
          1.2.1. Riot has created the League of Legends Global Esports Policies (the “LoL
                  Global Policies”) and the Rules to set forth certain policies, rules and
                  procedures that will apply to all Team Entities that have entered into the
                  Team Participation Agreements with the League Entity and have been
                  admitted by contract to compete in the League of the Americas.
          1.2.2. Standardized rules benefit all parties who are involved in the professional
                  play of LoL. The LoL Global Policies in addition to the Rules will apply to
                  and be binding on each of (i) the individual (natural person), entity and/or
                  group (“Owners”) who registered a Team to participate in the League of the
                  Americas (“Team”), and (ii) to each Team’s players (“Players”), coaches
                  (“Coaches”), managers, owners and other representatives (the foregoing
                  collectively, “Team Personnel”).
          1.2.3. These Rules are in addition to, and not in lieu of any supplementary rules
                  enforced in each of the leagues. In the event of a conflict between the
                  Rules and any supplementary rules, the provisions that are most protective
                  of Riot (as determined by Riot in its sole discretion) will govern.
      1.3.   Due Diligence Requirements
              Teams shall fulfill all due diligence requirements prior to their participation in the
              applicable Tier 2 League. Failure by the Teams to meet these requirements by the
              specified deadline set by Riot may result in disqualification or other penalties as
              determined by Riot.
      1.4.   Disciplinary Action
              Riot shall be entitled to undertake such disciplinary procedures as it determines
              in relation to any breach of, or failure to comply with, these Rules, any Owners or
              Team Personnel and to impose such fines, suspensions, disqualifications and
              other disciplinary actions (or combinations thereof) at the discretion of Riot
              (collectively, “Disciplinary Actions”); and such Disciplinary Actions (i) may be

      1.5.   Best Interests of the League
              Individuals charged to oversee and manage league operations may act with the
              necessary authority to preserve the best interests of the League (“League

              this document. League Officials may use any form of punitive actions at their

      1.6.   Finality of Decisions
              All decisions regarding the interpretation of these Rules, Player eligibility,
              scheduling and staging of the leagues, and penalties for misconduct, lie solely
              with the League, the decisions of which are final. League decisions with respect
              to these Rules cannot be appealed (except as set forth in the League Operating
              Manual)(“Manual”) and shall not give rise to any claim for monetary damages or
              any other legal or equitable remedy.
      1.7.   Official Language
              The official language of the Rules is English. All documents, communications,
              rulesets, policies, disciplinary proceedings, and competitive rulings will be written
              and communicated in English. In cases where translations are provided for
              convenience only, such translations are unofficial, and the English version shall
              prevail.

2. Format
      2.1.   Definitions
          2.1.1. Champion. A champion is a unique LoL playable character with distinct
                  abilities and stats.
          2.1.2. Coin Flip. A method for making a random choice between two options by
                  tossing a coin, either physically or digitally with the assistance of third-party
                  apps, with the Team choosing heads or tails first according to the
                  alphabetical order of the official Team name, excluding sponsors, and using
                  the result to decide the applicable outcome.
          2.1.3. Game. Game is an instance of League of Legends competition on the
                  Summoner’s Rift map.
          2.1.4. Local Time. The governing time zone will be the local time of the relevant
                  city detailed below:

         2.1.5. Match. Match is a set of Games that is played until one Team wins a
                 majority of the total Games. The winning Team will either receive a win tally
                 in a league format or advance to the next round in a tournament format. In a
                 best-of-one format, the terms Game and Match may be used
                 interchangeably. Best-of-one, best-of-three and best-of-five may be
                 abbreviated with Bo1, Bo3 and Bo5 respectively throughout the Rules. All
                 Matches in the NACL will be played as a Fearless Draft.
         2.1.6. Phase. A Phase is a distinct segment within a Split where Teams compete
                 according to a specific format, such as the Regular Season, Playoffs, or
                 other structured competitions. Each Phase serves a unique purpose, such
                 as establishing rankings, determining qualifications, or advancing Teams to
                 the next stage of the Split.
         2.1.7. Regular Season. A Regular Season involves Teams competing within the
                 League to establish rankings and determine spots in the Playoffs or other
                 Phases of the applicable Split.
         2.1.8. Season. A Season is the period of time during each year in the Term in
                 which League Games will take place, beginning on the date of the first
                 Preseason game in a given year and ending on the date of the last game of
                 the World Championships for such year.

         2.1.9. Split. A Split is a competitive stage within the Season where Teams
                 compete across different Phases, such as the Regular Season, Playoffs, or
                 other formats, to determine rankings, qualifications, and performance
                 outcomes.
        2.1.10. Right of Choice. The Right of Choice is a benefit granted to one of the
                 Teams in a Match. It allows said Team to choose either the order in which
                 Teams start Champion Select that Game(they choose to be first or second
                 pick) or the position of Teams in Summoner’s Rift that Game (they can
                 choose between red or blue). For any Game beyond the first Game of a
                 Match, the Right of Choice will be granted to the Team that lost the
                 previous Game.
        2.1.11. Round Robin. A Round Robin is a Phase of the Competition in which all
                 Teams face off against each other once.

      2.4.   Summer Split

          2.4.1. Summer Split Phases
                     Summer Split consists of two phases: the Regular Season and the Playoffs.
                     In the Regular Season, Teams will be drafted into two groups and compete
                     in a single round robin crossplay format, as well as a Last Chance Qualifier
                     (LCQ) if applicable. In the Playoffs, the top 4 teams from each group will
                     play in a double-elimination format, featuring a mix of Bo3 and Bo5
                     matches.
          2.4.2. Regular Season

                     In the Regular Season, Teams will be divided into Group A and Group B.
                     The 1st and 2nd seeded Teams from the Spring Split will draft Teams to

                     The Teams will continue alternating selections until both Groups consist of
                     five (5) Teams. Each Team will play one (1) Bo3 Match against each of the
                     Teams in the opposing Group. In the Regular Season, Teams will be ranked
                     in order of Match Wins. At the conclusion of the Regular Season, the top
                     three (3) Teams from each Group will automatically advance to the Playoffs.

      2.5.   Tiebreakers

          2.5.1. In the event that Teams are tied in the Standings after the conclusion of a
                  Round Robin, the tie will be broken using the following criteria, in this order:

                      ●   Head to Head
                      ●   Head to Head Game Score
                      ●   Strength of Victory (SoV)
                      ●   Weighted Strength of Victory (WSoV)
                      ●   Victory Time
                      ●   Coinflip

                           See Appendix 2 for details.

          2.5.2. All tiebreaker-games will be played as single Best-of-1 Games.

          2.5.3. Tiebreaker-games will be scheduled at the sole discretion of the League.

          2.5.4. For all tiebreaker-games the Right of Choice will be determined by the
                  head-to-head record of the teams playing the tiebreaker. If the teams have
                  not yet played against each other, a coin flip will instead determine the right
                  for side selection.

          2.5.5. Tiebreaker games will not be played if their outcome will not have any
                  competitive implications.

      2.6.   Right of Choice

          2.6.1. Teams who have been awarded the Right of Choice can either choose their
                  pick position on Champion Select (being the first or second pick) OR choose
                  their map side (being on the Blue or Red side of the Summoner’s Rift map).
                  This will be referred hereinafter as the “Choice”.

          2.6.2. The Team who does not have the Right of Choice in a Match will be able to
                  make the “Remaining Choice”. They will be able to choose either the pick
                  position or the map position, whichever was not chosen by the team with
                  the Right of Choice. As an example, if Team A is facing Team B, and Team
                  A has the Right of Choice, Team A could declare to League Officials that
                  they have chosen to be first pick. In this instance, Team B would have the
                  “Remaining Choice” between playing on either the Red or Blue Side of the
                  Summoner’s Rift map.

                          APPENDIX 2 - Tiebreaker Criteria
 1.   Match Score. The amount of Matches won by a team. Tiebreaker games are not
       included.
 2.   Game Score. The subtraction of the Games won minus the Games lost throughout
       the Regular Season.
 3.   Strength of Victory Score. This section sets forth the manner in which the league
       shall calculate a Team’s Strength of Victory Score (“SoV”).
       3.1. Wherever necessary, the Teams shall be ranked from 1 through 8 based
               upon Match winning percentage, where the highest winning percentage is
               ranked as 1, and each Team is ranked in descending order of Match winning
               percentage.
       3.2. In situations where exactly two Teams have the same Match winning
               percentage, the team who has the head-to-head advantage shall be
               considered the higher rank. When no Team in a two Team tie has the
               head-to-head advantage or in all situations where more than two Teams are
               tied, the tied Teams shall each be counted at the highest possible position.
       3.3. Where Teams are tied and assigned the same rank, the position of the next
               Team in descending Match winning percentage order shall be calculated by
               adding one to the number of Teams with a higher Match winning percentage
               than the Team being assigned a position.
               Note: For example, if two Teams are tied with a Match winning percentage of
               83.3% (.833) and are 1-1 against each other in head-to-head Match play,
               they would each be considered as the 1st place Team for the purpose of this
               calculation. The next Team(s) in descending order would be considered the
               3rd place Team for the purpose of this calculation.
       3.4.   Once the above order is determined, a victory against a Team is worth a set
               amount of points as set forth in the table below. To calculate a Team’s
               Strength of Victory, the number of Match wins against each Team is
               multiplied by that Team’s Multiplier, with the resulting products added
               together.
               Note: For example, if the 3rd place Team wins against the 1st place Team (4
               points), wins 2–0 against the 4th place Team (2.5 points), and loses 1-2
               against the 6th place team (not counted), their WSoV would be 6.5.
