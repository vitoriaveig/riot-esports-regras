  1.    Introduction and Acceptance
         1.1. Introduction
              1.1.1. These Set 13 General official rules (hereinafter, “Rules”) govern all of the
                       Days for the Set 13 events (hereinafter, “Tournaments”).
              1.1.2. These Rules have been designed to ensure the integrity of competitive play
                       of Teamfight Tactics (hereinafter, “Game”) in connection with the
                       Tournament, and are intended to ensure fair competition that complies with
                       our Code of Conduct, as defined below in Section 7.
              1.1.3. These Rules may be translated into other languages. In the event of any
                       conflict or inconsistency between any translated version of these Rules and
                       the English version, the English version shall prevail, govern, and control.
              1.1.4. Entry into this Tournament does not constitute entry into any other
                       tournament, competition, contest, or sweepstakes.
         1.2. Acceptance
              1.2.1. To participate in these Tournaments, each participant (hereinafter, “Player”)
                       must agree to follow these Rules at all times, including the Code of Conduct
                       in Section 7. A Player must accept these Rules as part of the Player
                       Acceptance form. By participating in any game or match that is part of the
                       Tournament, a Player is confirming that he or she has accepted these Rules.
         1.3. Enforcement
              1.3.1. GGTech and sanctioned Tournament Administrators (as defined below) will
                       have the primary responsibility to enforce these Rules for all Players and
                       may penalize Players for violation of these Rules, as further described in
                       Section 7.

  3.    Player Eligibility
         3.1. Riot employees, GGTech employees, and players who are not permanent residents
                of the Americas region are not eligible to play in the Tournament, with exceptions to
                the latter made for those with a work or student visa.
         3.2. Players must fill out the agreement form and sign the eligibility and release form
                provided by the GGTech Admin Team to compete.
         3.3. For snapshots that invite players to events, the tiebreaker that will be used if they
                are tied in LP is ‘Top 4%’ (of all their games in the current set). If tied to the second
                decimal place, the win rate will be used instead.
              3.3.1. For invitations to the Tactician Trials and Tactician's Cup, players must be
                        ranked at the time the snapshot is taken. There is no minimum number of
                        games required.

                scoreboard information. Please reach out to an administrator if you require
                accommodations to this rule.

                3 of Tactician’s Cup I, II and Golden Spatula.

              8.1.1. Players are responsible for their own internet connection. A remake can be

                                after losing they must tell an admin and wait for approval to leave.

              8.2.1. In any tournament played on the Tournament Realm can be paused if
                        needed by a player. If a player needs to get in contact with an admin, the
                        player can pause the game by typing “/pause”. In certain cases, an admin
                        may reach out to a player through the in-game client to initiate a pause.
                        Abuse of the pause feature will result in disciplinary action.
              8.2.2. A player may pause a game for up to 10 minutes, with each lobby limited to
                        a total pause time of 30 minutes. Once the lobby’s total pause limit is
                        reached, players must resume play unless Tournament Officials grant
                        additional time. Pausing beyond this time limit will be considered unfair play
                        and may result in penalties at the discretion of Tournament Officials.
                    8.2.2.1. Unpausing: The player who initiated the pause must be the one to
                                resume the game unless authorized by an admin for another player
                                to unpause it.
              8.2.3. Admins have full discretion and authority to adjust pause timers on a
                        case-by-case basis.

              8.3.1. The players who come first and second in each lobby are required to
                        screenshot the result of their lobby and submit it to admins, either on Discord
                        or the official tournament platform.

                        Time of the first game in a Tournament Stage, the next eligible player will be
                        invited to participate. If no eligible players are available, the lobby will

  9.    Code of Conduct
         9.1. Competitive Integrity
              9.1.1. Players are expected to play at their best at all times within any Match and to
                        avoid any behavior that potentially threatens the actual or perceived integrity
                        of competition or that is otherwise inconsistent with the principles of good
                        sportsmanship, honesty, or fair play. Violating this rule will be subject to
                        penalties at the discretion of the Tournament Administrators. All decisions
                        regarding violations are at the sole discretion of the League. The examples
                        below are listed for illustrative purposes only:
              9.1.2. Collusion. Defined as cooperation or conspiracy to cheat or deceive others.
                        The cooperation or conspiracy can occur among Players, Staff, and/or
                        Organizations, and can be done to the sole benefit of the parties involved in
                        the cooperation or conspiracy. The list of conspirators is not exhaustive.
                        Collusion includes, but is not limited to, acts such as:
                    9.1.2.1. Soft Play is defined as any agreement among two (2) or more
                                players to not damage, impede or otherwise play to a reasonable
                                standard of competition in a game.
                    9.1.2.2. Pre-arranging to split the prize money and/or any other form of
                                compensation.
                    9.1.2.3. Sending or receiving signals, electronic or otherwise, from outside
                                sources to/from a Player and/or Staff.
                    9.1.2.4. Deliberately losing a Game for compensation, or any other reason, or
                                attempting to induce another player and/or Staff to do so.
              9.1.3. Hacking. Any modification to the game creates unfairness or an advantage.
              9.1.4. Exploiting. Intentionally using any in-game bug to an advantage.
              9.1.5. Ghosting. It is defined as looking at, looking at, or attempting to look at
                        spectator monitors and opposing players’ screen(s)/game(s).

                       ostracize a person and/or affect the dignity of the person.
               9.2.3. Sexual Harassment. Sexual harassment is forbidden. Sexual harassment is

                       whether a reasonable person would regard the conduct as undesirable or

                  6.2.1.1. Soft play, which is defined as any agreement among two(2) or more

                              compensation.
                  6.2.1.3. Sending or receiving signals, electronic or otherwise, from a
                              confederate to/from a player.
                  6.2.1.4. Deliberately losing a game for compensation, or for any other reason,
                              or attempting to induce another player to do so.
