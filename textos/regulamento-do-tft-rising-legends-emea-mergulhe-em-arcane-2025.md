                                     EMEA Esports Rulebook

The Teamfight Tactics Global Player Policy rules apply to the Teamfight Tactics EMEA Esports
Tournament Series, as well as the additional rules and regulations set out below.
The English language version of these rules supersedes any other translations.

All players who participate in the EMEA Esports Tournament Series are automatically under the
jurisdiction of the Global Player Policy, the TFT Code of Conduct, the Esports Global Code of
Conduct and the Riot Games Terms of Service.

    1.   General Terms
          1.1.     EMEA Golden Spatula - GS
                  1.1.1. The final competition of TFT EMEA Esports. Most or all players who will
                          qualify to the Tactician’s Crown will qualify from this tournament.

          1.2.     Tactician’s Cups - TC
                  1.2.1. The premier 3-day closed tournament of EMEA Esports. Players that will
                           participate in a TC will come from: TFT Regional Competitions, Tactician
                           Trials, and Cup Snapshot

          1.3.     Tactician Trials - TT
                  1.3.1. Prior to each TC, a Tactician Trials will be held to qualify players to fill out
                           the remaining slots in a TC.

          1.4.     TFT Regional Competitions (TRC)
                  1.4.1. Riot recognized local region-focused competitions. Each recognized TRC
                          will feed their current Champion or #1 player directly into the next
                          Tactician’s Cup.

          1.5.     Ladder Snapshots
                  1.5.1. Depending on a player’s rank in the overall official TFT ladder, snapshot
                          points will be awarded. Snapshots take place at certain dates throughout
                          the competitive set’s competition calendar.

          1.6.     Time Zones & Start Times
                  1.6.1. Tournament operations start times, snapshots, and deadlines are listed in
                          CET/CEST format. Depending on the time of the year, CET (Central
                          European Time) or CEST (Central European Summer Time) will be
                          followed. Check the Discord Server for the most up to date time &
                          information.

 2.    Eligibility
        2.1.     Regions
                2.1.1. The regions eligible to compete in TFT EMEA Esports are: Europe, CIS,
                        Middle East, Türkiye, and Africa.

                2.1.2.   Regions are further divided into sub-regions, countries, and or territories.
                          Refer to the Global Player Policy and the TFT Regional Competition
                          sections for specific region breakdown.

                2.1.3.   Riot Games and licensed tournament operators reserve the right to ask
                          for Proof of Residence or Proof of Age at any time.

                2.1.4.   A player must be a resident of one of the eligible regions to participate in
                          the tournament.

        2.2.     Residency
                2.2.1. A player must be a legal resident of the region they would like to
                        participate in.

                2.2.2.   A player who has lived at least 13 years of their life within a competitive
                          region is eligible to participate in their region’s tournament.
                      2.2.2.1. A player may keep their residency should they move to another
                                  region, so long as they can prove their previous residency.

                2.2.3.   A player who has recently moved to a region must live at least four
                          calendar months in said region to be able to participate in that region’s
                          tournament.

                2.2.4.   In general, a player may only participate in one regional tournament at a
                          time.

                2.2.5.   Riot Games and licensed tournament operators may request for Proof of
                          Residence or Proof of Age at any time.

        2.3.     Ladder Snapshots
                2.3.1. Servers:
                      2.3.1.1. Residents of the EUW and EUNE servers MUST play in one of
                                those two servers.
                      2.3.1.2. Residents of the RU, TR, or ME regions may play in the server of
                                the region they are a resident of, or in the EUW or EUNE servers.
                      2.3.1.3. Residents of the Africa region may play on the EUW or EUNE or
                                ME server.
                      2.3.1.4. This server cannot be changed for the duration of the Tournament
                                Series/Set.

        4.1.     Ladder Snapshots
                4.1.1. Ladder Snapshots will be taken at 21:59:59 CET/CEST on Tuesdays.

                          server in between each Snapshot to be eligible for the next one. The first
                          snapshot has no game requirement.

                          started before and finished after will not.

                          Snapshot. Players with the same LP in a Ladder Snapshot will receive the
                          same amount of points.

 6.    Tiebreakers
        6.1.     General Tiebreakers
                6.1.1. Any players that are tied on points for any of the following - Reseeding
                          Lobbies, End of day cut offs, final tournament position - will be
                          differentiated in the following manner:
                      6.1.1.1. Total Tournament Points
                      6.1.1.2. Highest number of wins and Top 4s in the tournament stage (wins
                                  are counted twice).
                      6.1.1.3. Highest number of placements for each position achievable in a
                                  lobby (1st, 2nd, 3rd, etc.) in the tournament stage.
                      6.1.1.4. Finishing position in the most recent game in the tournament
                                  stage followed by position in each previous game (i.e. Game 5, 4,
                                  3, etc.)

        6.2.     Reseeding
                6.2.1.   For reseeding between lobbies, if players are unable to be differentiated
                          by the above methods, they will be sorted randomly between the positions
                          they are tied for.

        6.3.     Qualifier Points
                6.3.1.   If players are tied on Qualifier Points the tie will be broken through the
                          following methods:
                      6.3.1.1. Number of Tactician's Cups attended.
                      6.3.1.2. Placement in the most recent Qualifier Point event.
                      6.3.1.3. Top 4 rate in Tactician’s Cups.

                6.3.2.   For the TFT Tactician’s Crown qualification, ties will be broken based on
                          placement on Day 2 of the EMEA Golden Spatula.

        6.4.     Cup Snapshot
                6.4.1.   If players are tied on LPs when the Cup Snapshot has occurred, the tie
                          will be broken through the following methods:
                      6.4.1.1. Highest Top 4 rate
                      6.4.1.2. Highest Win rate

        6.5.     Ladder Snapshot Ranking
                6.5.1.   If players are tied on Ladder Snapshot Points when the last Snapshot has
                          occurred, the tie will be broken through the following methods:
                      6.5.1.1. Peak rank in a given snapshot across the set.
                      6.5.1.2. Highest rank in the most recent snapshot.
