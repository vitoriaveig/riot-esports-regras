1. Introduction
      1.1.   Background
              Riot Games, Inc. (“RGI”) and/or its affiliate companies (collectively referred to as
              “Riot,” “Riot Entities,” or individually as a “Riot Entity”) have established the
              League of the Americas Ruleset (“Rules”) for competitive play of League of
              Legends (“LoL”) in the Americas region. These Rules are designed solely to
              ensure the integrity of the League Championship Series (“LCS”), with “League
              Entity” referring to the applicable Riot Entity in each contract.
      1.2.   Application of the Rules
          1.2.1. Riot has created the League of Legends Global Esports Policies (the “LoL
                  Global Policies”) and the Rules to set forth certain policies, rules and
                  procedures that will apply to all Team Entities that have entered into the
                  Team Participation Agreements with the League Entity and have been
                  admitted by contract to compete in the League of the Americas.
          1.2.2. Standardized rules benefit all parties who are involved in the professional
                  play of LoL. The LoL Global Policies in addition to the Rules will apply to
                  and be binding on each of (i) the individual (natural person), entity and/or
                  group (“Owners”) who registered a Team to participate in the League of the
                  Americas (“Team”), and (ii) to each Team’s players (“Players”), coaches
                  (“Coaches”), managers, owners and other representatives (the foregoing
                  collectively, “Team Personnel”).
          1.2.3. These Rules are in addition to, and not in lieu of any supplementary rules
                  enforced in each of the leagues. In the event of a conflict between the
                  Rules and any supplementary rules, the provisions that are most protective
                  of Riot (as determined by Riot in its sole discretion) will govern.
      1.3.   Due Diligence Requirements
              Teams shall fulfill all due diligence requirements prior to their participation in the LCS.
              Failure by the Teams to meet these requirements by the specified deadline set by
              Riot may result in disqualification or other penalties as determined by Riot.
      1.4.   Disciplinary Action
              Riot shall be entitled to undertake such disciplinary procedures as it determines
              in relation to any breach of, or failure to comply with, these Rules, any Owners or
              Team Personnel and to impose such fines, suspensions, disqualifications and
              other disciplinary actions (or combinations thereof) at the discretion of Riot
              (collectively, “Disciplinary Actions”); and such Disciplinary Actions (i) may be
              publicly disclosed by Riot, and (ii) are reasonable and necessary in order to

      1.5.   Best Interests of the League

              All decisions regarding the interpretation of these Rules, Player eligibility,
              scheduling and staging of the leagues, and penalties for misconduct, lie solely
              with the League, the decisions of which are final. League decisions with respect
              to these Rules cannot be appealed (except as set forth in the League Operating
              Manual)(“Manual”) and shall not give rise to any claim for monetary damages or
              any other legal or equitable remedy.

              and communicated in English. In cases where translations are provided for
              convenience only, such translations are unofficial, and the English version shall
              prevail.

      2.1.   Definitions

                  abilities and stats.

                  alphabetical order of the official Team name, excluding sponsors, and using
                  the result to decide the applicable outcome.
          2.1.3. Game. A Game is an instance of League of Legends competition on the
                  Summoner’s Rift map.

                  city detailed below:

         2.1.5. Match. Match is a set of Games that is played until one Team wins a
                 majority of the total Games. The winning Team will either receive a win tally
                 in a league format or advance to the next round in a tournament format. In a
                 best-of-one format, the terms Game and Match may be used
                 interchangeably. Best-of-one, best-of-three and best-of-five may be
                 abbreviated with Bo1, Bo3 and Bo5 respectively throughout the Rules. All
                 Matches in the LCS will be played as a Fearless Draft.
         2.1.6. Phase. A Phase is a distinct segment within a Split where Teams compete
                 according to a specific format, such as the Regular Season, Playoffs, or
                 other structured competitions. Each Phase serves a unique purpose, such
                 as establishing rankings, determining qualifications, or advancing Teams to
                 the next stage of the Split.
         2.1.7. Regular Season. A Regular Season involves Teams competing within the
                 League to establish rankings and determine spots in the Playoffs or other
                 Phases of the applicable Split.
         2.1.8. Season. A Season is the period of time during each year in the Term in
                 which League Games will take place, beginning on the date of the first
                 Preseason game in a given year and ending on the date of the last game of
                 the World Championships for such year.

         2.1.9. Split. A Split is a competitive stage within the Season where Teams
                 compete across different Phases, such as the Regular Season, Playoffs, or
                 other formats, to determine rankings, qualifications, and performance
                 outcomes.
        2.1.10. Right of Choice. The Right of Choice is a benefit granted to one of the
                 Teams in a Match. It allows said Team to choose either the order in which
                 Teams start Champion Select that Game(they choose to be first or second
                 pick) or the position of Teams in Summoner’s Rift that Game (they can
                 choose between red or blue). For any Game beyond the first Game of a
                 Match, the Right of Choice will be granted to the Team that lost the
                 previous Game.
        2.1.11. Round Robin. A Round Robin is a Phase of the Competition in which all
                 Teams face off against each other once.

      2.5.   Tiebreakers
          2.5.1. For the Teams tied in Match Score in the Swiss Stage, the tie will be broken
                  using the following criteria, in this order:

                     ●   Game Score
                     ●   Strength of Victory (SoV)
                     ●   Weighted Strength of Victory (WSoV)
                     ●   Victory Time
                     ●   Coinflip

                          See Appendix 2 for details.

          2.5.2. In the event that Teams are tied in the Standings after the conclusion of a
                  Round Robin, the tie will be broken using the following criteria, in this order:

                     ●   Head to Head
                     ●   Head to Head Game Score
                     ●   Strength of Victory (SoV)
                     ●   Weighted Strength of Victory (WSoV)
                     ●   Victory Time
                     ●   Coinflip

                          See Appendix 2 for details.

          2.5.3. When applicable, tiebreaker-games will be played as single Best-of-1
                  Games.

          2.5.4. Tiebreaker-games will be scheduled at the sole discretion of the League.

          2.5.5. For all tiebreaker games the Right of Choice (see 2.1.10 “Right of Choice”)
                   will be determined by the head-to-head record of the Teams playing the
                   tiebreaker. If the teams have not yet played against each other, a coin flip
                   will instead determine the Right of Choice.

      2.6.   Right of Choice

          2.6.1. Teams who have been awarded the Right of Choice can either choose their
                  pick position on Champion Select (being the first or second pick) OR
                  choose their map side (being on the Blue or Red side of the Summoner’s
                  Rift map). This will be referred hereinafter as the “Choice”.

          2.6.2. The Team who does not have the Right of Choice in a Match will be able to
                  make the “Remaining Choice”. They will be able to choose either the pick
                  position or the map position, whichever was not chosen by the team with
                  the Right of Choice. As an example, if Team A is facing Team B, and Team
                  A has the Right of Choice, Team A could declare to League Officials that
                  they have chosen to be first pick. In this instance, Team B would have the
                  “Remaining Choice” between playing on either the Red or Blue Side of the
                  Summoner’s Rift map.

          2.6.3. Both the Choice and the Remaining Choice need to be made
                  simultaneously. When the deadline set by League Officials arrives, the Team
                  with the Choice will announce their decision to League Officials, and the
                  Team that is entitled to the Remaining Choice must announce both their
                  preferred side and preferred picking order to League Officials, with the
                  understanding only one of those will be possible (whichever one hasn’t
                  been chosen by the Team with the Right of Choice).

          2.6.4. The Choice and the Remaining Choice will be announced simultaneously.

          2.6.5. In a Match with multiple Games, the Right of Choice for Game 2 onwards
                  will be given to the Team that has lost the previous Game in that Match.

      2.7.   Competitive Patch & New Champions

          2.7.1. Competitive Patch
              All Games will be played on the current patch available on the live service (“Live
              Service”). Changes to the competitive patch will be at the discretion of the
              League. The competitive patch will be updated after its release onto the live
              server. A patch will not be implemented if a week of Games or a playoff round
              has started. Example: Patch X.X was released on 11:59pm, February 5. It will be
              eligible to be used as a competitive patch for all Matches on or after 11:59pm,
              February 5 unless a week of Matches or a playoff round has started.

      3.3.   Player Age
              A Player must be at least seventeen (17) years of age to be considered eligible to
              compete on an Active Roster in Tier 1 competitions by the time of their first
              match. For Tier 2, the minimum age requirement is sixteen (16) years of age. If
              there are regional laws that prevent Players below the ages listed above from
              playing League of Legends, the minimum age according to the law will instead be
              the minimum age for the Competition.
      3.4.   Ecosystem Changes in Residency
          3.4.1. OCE Residents and IMP Residents

                      Due to changes within the League of Legends ecosystem, the Players listed
                      in Appendix 3 under OCE Residents and IMP Residents are considered
                      Residents of North America.

          3.4.2. LTA Grandfathering
                  Players who have an existing contract under the previous “Americas
                  Resident” slot, or signed one in 2025 and choose to sign with another org
                  in the same region/extend their 1 year contract, will be grandfathered as a
                  temporary resident of that Region (for example a North American Player
                  who played in Brazil in 2025 and chose to remain competing in Brazil in
                  2026 would be considered a Resident in the Brazil region until they return to
                  North America).

          3.4.3. Players from the LATAM Region will be regarded as Residents in both the
                  Brazil and the North American Regions in 2026 and 2027. Beyond that, they
                  will need to choose one of the Regions to claim residency in, and will no
                  longer be considered Residents of the other.

11. Chronobreak and Game Stoppage
   11.1.   Definitions
        11.1.1. Chronobreak. Chronobreak is a tool used to rollback the state of a Game
                 to a previous time, in the event that League Officials determine the integrity
                 of a Game has been compromised.
        11.1.2. Cost. Cost is any (i) Player character deaths; (ii) objectives (Tower, Inhibitor,
                 Dragon, Rift Herald, or Baron) taken that were not otherwise in progress at
                 the Dead-Ball State (i.e., dragon was pulled or three Players on the tower
                 with a minion wave and no opposition); or (iii) ultimate, item or summoner
                 spell usage where such ultimate spell at Rank 1 (regardless of the ultimate
                 rank or cooldown at the time of the bug) or base summoner spell or item
                 (i.e., without cooldown reductions from masteries or items) has a cooldown
                 of 110 seconds or longer. An ultimate or summoner spell used, in the
                 opinion of League Officials, outside of normal play patterns for the purpose
                 of triggering a Cost under this rule shall not constitute a Cost. Other factors
                 such as vision (placed or destroyed wards), minions farmed, etc., while all
                 carrying some value in the Game, do not rise to the level of consideration in
                 whether to use Chronobreak.
        11.1.3. Critical Bug. Critical Bug is a Bug (including an Unintentional Hardware
                 Failure) that significantly damages a Player’s ability to compete in the Game
                 situation, significantly alters Game stats or gameplay mechanics, or a
                 situation in which the external environmental conditions become untenable.
        11.1.4. Dead-Ball State. Dead-Ball State is a point in a Game when neither Team
                 is heavily engaged with one another, although some minor engagement
                 may still constitute a Dead-Ball State.
        11.1.5. Game of Record. A Game where all ten Players have loaded and which
                  has progressed to a point of meaningful interaction between opposing
                  Teams. Once a Game attains Game Of Record (“GOR”) status, the period
                  ends in which incidental restarts may be permitted and a Game will be
                  considered as “official” from that point onward. Examples of conditions
                  which establish GOR:

                            ●   Any attack or ability is landed on minions, jungle creeps,
                                 structures, or enemy Champions.

                                 opponent’s jungle by either Team, which includes either leaving
                                 the river or entering brush connected to enemy jungle.
                            ●   Game timer reaches two minutes (00:02:00).
      11.1.6. Minor Bug. Minor Bug is a Bug (including an Unintentional Hardware
               Failure) that is, at worst, inconvenient to Players. This may include Bugs

               optimal, can be played through if necessary. For the absence of doubt, in a

      11.1.7. Play Through Bug. A Play Through Bug is a Bug that does not significantly

                  i.e., those Bugs that are provided to Teams in advance of Games (usually
                  champion, item or environment interactions and persistent effects) for

                  avoided or mitigated through any means other than disabling of the
                  offending champions, skins or items and thus remakes are not available for
                  these Bugs.

                  Bug has a high impact on the competitive integrity of the Game. League

                  determine that the disadvantaged Player or Team intended to or
                  purposefully triggered the Bug. In a Chronobreak situation, League Officials
                  shall treat the Play Through Bug as a Minor Bug or Critical Bug, as

                          APPENDIX 2 - Tiebreaker Criteria
 1.   Match Score. The amount of Matches won by a team. Tiebreaker games are not
       included.
 2.   Game Score. The subtraction of the Games won minus the Games lost throughout
       the Regular Season.
 3.   Strength of Victory Score. This section sets forth the manner in which the league
       shall calculate a Team’s Strength of Victory Score (“SoV”).
       3.1. Wherever necessary, the Teams shall be ranked from 1 through 8 based
               upon Match winning percentage, where the highest winning percentage is
               ranked as 1, and each Team is ranked in descending order of Match winning
               percentage.
       3.2. In situations where exactly two Teams have the same Match winning
               percentage, the team who has the head-to-head advantage shall be
               considered the higher rank. When no Team in a two Team tie has the
               head-to-head advantage or in all situations where more than two Teams are
               tied, the tied Teams shall each be counted at the highest possible position.
       3.3. Where Teams are tied and assigned the same rank, the position of the next
               Team in descending Match winning percentage order shall be calculated by
               adding one to the number of Teams with a higher Match winning percentage
               than the Team being assigned a position.
               Note: For example, if two Teams are tied with a Match winning percentage of
               83.3% (.833) and are 1-1 against each other in head-to-head Match play,
               they would each be considered as the 1st place Team for the purpose of this
               calculation. The next Team(s) in descending order would be considered the
               3rd place Team for the purpose of this calculation.
       3.4.   Once the above order is determined, a victory against a Team is worth a set
               amount of points as set forth in the table below. To calculate a Team’s
               Strength of Victory, the number of Match wins against each Team is
               multiplied by that Team’s Multiplier, with the resulting products added
               together.
               Note: For example, if the 3rd place Team wins against the 1st place Team (4
               points), wins 2–0 against the 4th place Team (2.5 points), and loses 1-2
               against the 6th place team (not counted), their WSoV would be 6.5.
