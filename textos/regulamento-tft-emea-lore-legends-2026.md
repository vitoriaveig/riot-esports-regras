                                 EMEA Esports Rulebook

The Teamfight Tactics Global Player Policy rules apply to the Teamfight Tactics EMEA Esports
Tournament Series, as well as the additional rules and regulations set out below.
The English language version of these rules supersedes any other translations.

All players who participate in the EMEA Esports Tournament Series are automatically under the
jurisdiction of the Global Player Policy, the TFT Code of Conduct, the Esports Global Code of
Conduct and the Riot Games Terms of Service.

 1.    General Terms
        1.1.   Tactician’s Crown (TacCrown)
                   ■ The pinnacle of TFT Esports: the Global Championship, hosted at the end
                       of every Set.

        1.2.   TFT Pro Circuit (TPC)
                   ■ The official Tier 1 competition of TFT Esports. Each competitive region
                      has their own TPC.

        1.3.   EMEA Regional Finals (RF)
                  ■ The final competition of TFT EMEA Esports. Most or all players who will
                     qualify to the Tactician’s Crown will qualify from this tournament.

        1.4.   Tactician’s Cups (TC)
                   ■ A 3-day closed tournament of EMEA Esports. Players that will participate
                       in a TC will come from: Tactician Trials, Cup Snapshots, or previous TC
                       top players.

        1.5.   Tactician Trials - (TT)
                   ■ Prior to each TC, a Tactician Trials will be held to qualify players to fill out
                       the remaining slots in a TC.

        1.6.   Ladder Snapshots
                   ■ Depending on a player’s rank in the overall official TFT ladder, snapshot
                      points will be awarded. Snapshots take place at certain dates throughout
                      the competitive set’s competition calendar.

        1.7.   Time Zones & Start Times
                   ■ Tournament operations start times, snapshots, and deadlines are listed in
                      CET/CEST format. Depending on the time of the year, CET (Central
                      European Time) or CEST (Central European Summer Time) will be
                      followed. Check the Discord Server for the most up to date time &
                      information.

 2.    Eligibility
        2.1.   Regions
                   ■ The regions eligible to compete in TFT EMEA Esports are: Europe, CIS,
                      Middle East, Türkiye, and Africa.

                      ■ Regions are further divided into sub-regions, countries, and or territories.
                         Refer to the Global Player Policy specific region breakdown.

                      ■ Riot Games and licensed tournament operators reserve the right to ask
                         for Proof of Residence or Proof of Age at any time.

                      ■ A player must be a resident of one of the eligible regions to participate in
                         the tournament.

        2.2.   Residency
                   ■ A player must be a legal resident of the region they would like to
                      participate in.

                      ■ A player who has lived at least 13 years of their life within a competitive
                         region is eligible to participate in their region’s tournament.
                             ● A player may keep their residency should they move to another
                                 region, so long as they can prove their previous residency.

                      ■ A player who has recently moved to a region must live at least four
                         calendar months in said region to be able to participate in that region’s
                         tournament.

                      ■ In general, a player may only participate in one pan-regional tournament
                         at a time.

                      ■ Riot Games and licensed tournament operators may request for Proof of
                         Residence or Proof of Age at any time.

        2.3.   Ladder Snapshots
                   ■ Servers:
                         ● Residents of the EUW and EUNE servers MUST play in one of
                             those two servers.
                         ● Residents of the TR region may play in the TR, EUW or EUNE
                             servers.
                         ● Residents of regions/countries not outlined above may play on
                             either the EUW or EUNE server.
                         ● This server cannot be changed for the duration of the Tournament
                             Series/Set.

 6.    Tiebreakers
        6.1.   General Tiebreakers
                  ■ Any players that are tied on points for any of the following - Reseeding
                      Lobbies, End of day cut offs, final tournament position - will be
                      differentiated in the following manner:
                           ● Total Tournament Points
                           ● Highest number of wins and Top 4s in the tournament stage (wins
                              are counted twice).
                           ● Highest number of placements for each position achievable in a
                              lobby (1st, 2nd, 3rd, etc.) in the tournament stage.
                           ● Finishing position in the most recent game in the tournament
                              stage followed by position in each previous game (i.e. Game 5, 4,
                              3, etc.)

        6.2.   Reseeding
                    ■ For reseeding between lobbies, if players are unable to be differentiated
                       by the above methods, they will be sorted randomly between the positions
                       they are tied for.
