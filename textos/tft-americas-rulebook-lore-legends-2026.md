  1.    Introduction and Acceptance
         1.1. Introduction
              1.1.1. These Set 16 General official rules (hereinafter, “Rules”) govern all of the
                       Stages for the Set 16 events (hereinafter, “Tournaments”).
              1.1.2. These Rules have been designed to ensure the integrity of competitive play
                       of Teamfight Tactics (hereinafter, “Game”) in connection with the
                       Tournament, and are intended to ensure fair competition that complies with
                       our Code of Conduct, as defined below in Section 7.
              1.1.3. These Rules may be translated into other languages. In the event of any
                       conflict or inconsistency between any translated version of these Rules and
                       the English version, the English version shall prevail, govern, and control.
              1.1.4. Entry into this Tournament does not constitute entry into any other
                       tournament, competition, contest, or sweepstakes.
         1.2. Acceptance
              1.2.1. To participate in these Tournaments, each participant (hereinafter, “Player”)
                       must agree to follow these Rules at all times, including the Code of Conduct
                       in Section 7. A Player must accept these Rules as part of the Player
                       Acceptance form. By participating in any game or match that is part of the
                       Tournament, a Player is confirming that he or she has accepted these Rules.
         1.3. Enforcement
              1.3.1. GGTech and sanctioned Tournament Administrators (as defined below) will
                       have the primary responsibility to enforce these Rules for all Players and
                       may penalize Players for violation of these Rules, as further described in
                       Section 7.

  3.    Player Eligibility
         3.1. Riot employees, GGTech employees, and players who are not permanent residents
                of the Americas region are not eligible to play in the Tournament, with exceptions to
                the latter made for those with a work or student visa.
         3.2. Players must fill out the agreement form and sign the eligibility and release form
                provided by the GGTech Admin Team to compete.
         3.3. For snapshots that invite players to events, the tiebreaker that will be used if they
                are tied in LP is ‘Top 4%’ (of all their games in the current set). If tied to the second
                decimal place, the win rate will be used instead.
              3.3.1. For invitations to the Tactician's Trials and Tactician's Cup, players must be
                        ranked at the time the snapshot is taken. There is no minimum number of
                        games required.
              3.3.2. Cup Snapshots will be taken at 9:00 PM PDT/PST on the following
                        Tuesdays:
                                     ● 1/6/2026

         3.4. Players who qualify for Day 1, 2, & 3 of either Tactician’s Cup 1, Tactician’s Cup 2,
                or Regional Finals are encouraged to stream their gameplay with a
                GGTech-provided overlay. A chat command will also be provided, which includes
                event information and scoreboard information. Please reach out to an administrator
                if you require accommodations for this rule.
         3.5. Players will be required to be on camera for the duration of their games during Day
                3 of Tactician’s Cup 1, 2, and all days of the Regional Finals.
              3.5.1. The primary use of player cameras is for competitive integrity and to ensure
                        players are at all times adhering to fair play.
              3.5.2. Player cameras will also be used for the main broadcast and will be captured
                        on VODs; by participating in the competition, players consent to the use of
                        their webcam feed for such purposes.
              3.5.3. Players are expected to test video sharing ahead of time to avoid any
                        possible issues during games.
              3.5.4. Tournament Officials will verify that cameras show acceptable views of
                        players and their surroundings before their games.

         6.3. Cascade System
              6.3.1. The Cascade System governs how duplicate qualifications to the Regional
                        Finals are resolved
                    6.3.1.1. Better qualification takes priority
                          6.3.1.1.1. If a player qualifies for both Play-Ins and Week 1, the Week 1
                                        qualification shall take precedence.
                          6.3.1.1.2. When a player qualifies for Week 1 and later for Play-Ins,
                                        they are skipped over on the Play-Ins qualification(s)
                    6.3.1.2. Chronological priority applies for the same event
                          6.3.1.2.1. If a player qualifies for the same more than once, the earlier
                                        qualification takes priority
                          6.3.1.2.2. Priority order is based on the event’s conclusion date
                    6.3.1.3. Players who qualify for the same stage multiple times are skipped
                                over on the more recent qualification(s)
                    6.3.1.4. When a player qualifies for Play-Ins and later for Week 1, their earlier
                                Play-Ins slot is reassigned to Tactician’s Cup 2.
                          6.3.1.4.1. Example: A player gets 10th in Tactician’s Cup I (Play-Ins),
                                        then the 1st in Ladder Snapshots (Week 1). In this case, 11th
                                        place in Cup 2 will qualify for Play-Ins.

  6.    Player Conduct
         6.1. Code of Conduct
                      Players are required to comply with the Riot Games Esports Global Code of
                      Conduct, incorporated herein by reference. In the event of conflict between
                      this Global Player Policy and the Riot Games Esports Global Code of
                      Conduct, Riot shall determine which policy applies (in its sole discretion).
         6.2. Competition Conduct
                      Collusion. Collusion is prohibited. Collusion is defined as any agreement
                      among two (2) or more players to disadvantage opposing players. Collusion
                      includes, but is not limited to, acts such as:
                  6.2.1.1. Soft play, which is defined as any agreement among two(2) or more
                              players to not damage, impede or otherwise play to a reasonable
                              standard of competition in a game.
                  6.2.1.2. Pre-arranging to split prize money and/or any other form of
                              compensation.
                  6.2.1.3. Sending or receiving signals, electronic or otherwise, from a
                              confederate to/from a player.
                  6.2.1.4. Deliberately losing a game for compensation, or for any other reason,
                              or attempting to induce another player to do so.
