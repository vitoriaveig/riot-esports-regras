                                   APAC Esports Rulebook

The Teamfight Tactics Global Player Policy rules apply to the Teamfight Tactics APAC Esports
Tournament Series, as well as the additional rules and regulations set out below.
The English language version of these rules supersedes any other translations.

All players who participate in the APAC Esports Tournament Series are automatically under the
jurisdiction of the Global Player Policy, the TFT Code of Conduct, the Esports Global Code of
Conduct and the Riot Games Terms of Service.

 1.​    General Terms
        1.1.​     APAC Regional Finals - GS
                1.1.1.​ The final competition of TFT APAC Esports. Most or all players who will
                        qualify to the Tactician’s Crown will qualify from this tournament.

        1.2.​     Tactician’s Cups - TC
                1.2.1.​ The premier 3-day closed tournament of APAC Esports. Players that will
                         participate in a TC will come from: Tactician Trials, Cup Snapshots, or
                         either previous TC top players or Top SB teams.

        1.3.​     Tactician Trials - TT
                1.3.1.​ Prior to each TC, a Tactician Trials will be held to qualify players to fill out
                         the remaining slots in a TC.

        1.4.​     Ladder Snapshots
                1.4.1.​ Depending on a player’s rank in the overall official TFT ladder, snapshot
                        points will be awarded. Snapshots take place at certain dates throughout
                        the competitive set’s competition calendar.

        1.5.​     Time Zones & Start Times
                1.5.1.​ Tournament operations start times, snapshots, and deadlines are
                        listed in GMT+8 format. Check the Discord Server for the most up to
                        date time & information.

 2.​    Eligibility
        2.1.​     Regions
                2.1.1.​ The regions eligible to compete in TFT APAC Esports are: Oceania,
                        South Korea, South East Asia, and Japan.

                2.1.2.​   Regions are further divided into sub-regions, countries, and or territories.
                          Refer to the Global Player Policy specific region breakdown.

                2.1.3.​   Riot Games and licensed tournament operators reserve the right to ask
                          for Proof of Residence or Proof of Age at any time.

 6.​    Tiebreakers
        6.1.​     General Tiebreakers
                6.1.1.​ Any players that are tied on points for any of the following - Reseeding
                        Lobbies, End of day cut offs, final tournament position - will be
                        differentiated in the following manner:
                             ○​ Total Tournament Points
                             ○​ Highest number of wins and Top 4s in the tournament stage (wins
                                are counted twice).
                             ○​ Highest number of placements for each position achievable in a
                                lobby (1st, 2nd, 3rd, etc.) in the tournament stage.
                             ○​ Finishing position in the most recent game in the tournament
                                stage followed by position in each previous game (i.e. Game 5, 4,
                                3, etc.)

        6.2.​     Reseeding
                6.2.1.​   For reseeding between lobbies, if players are unable to be differentiated
                          by the above methods, they will be sorted randomly between the positions
                          they are tied for.

        6.3.​     Ranked Ladder & Ladder Snapshot Tiebreaker
                6.3.1.​   If players are tied on LPs when the Cup / Ladder Snapshot has occurred,
                          the tie will be broken through the following methods:
                               ○​ Highest Top 4 rate
                               ○​ Highest Win rate

        6.4.​     Ladder Snapshot Points Ranking
                6.4.1.​   If players are tied on Ladder Snapshot Points when the last Snapshot has
                          occurred, the tie will be broken through the following methods:
                               ○​ Peak rank in a given snapshot across the set.
                               ○​ Highest rank in the most recent snapshot.

        7.2.​     Cascade System (Ladder - Tactician’s Cup - Regional Finals Relationship)
                7.2.1.​ The Cascade System governs how duplicate qualifications to the
                        Regional Finals are resolved
                           ○​ Better qualification takes priority
                                  ○​ If a player qualifies for both Play-Ins and Week 1, the
                                      Week 1 qualification shall take precedence.
                                  ○​ When a player qualifies for Week 1 and later for Play-Ins,
                                      they are skipped over on the Play-Ins qualification(s)
                           ○​ Chronological priority applies for the same event
                                  ○​ If a player qualifies for the same more than once, the
                                      earlier qualification takes priority

        9.8.​     General Conduct
                9.8.1.​ All players who participate in the TFT APAC Esports Tournament Series
                        fall automatically under the Code of Conduct detailed below as well as the
                        Player Conduct Rules set out in section VI. of the Global Player Policy.
                9.8.2.​ Any violation of the Code of Conduct will result in disciplinary action.
                        Disciplinary action will be subject to an investigation, unless immediate
                        action is deemed necessary by TFT APAC Esports Admins.
                9.8.3.​ If TFT APAC Esports Admins determine that a player has violated the
                        Summoner’s Code, the Teamfight Tactics Terms of Use, or other rules of
                        Teamfight Tactics, TFT APAC Esports Admins may assign penalties at
                        their sole discretion.
                9.8.4.​ If a TFT APAC Esports Admin contacts a player to discuss the
                        investigation, the player is obligated to tell the truth. If a player withholds
                        information or misleads a TFT APAC Esports Official creating an
                        obstruction of the investigation then the player is subject to punishment.

        9.9.​     If a player is banned or suspended on the Live server, the player is automatically
                  ineligible to participate in any TFT APAC Esports tournament or event for the
                  duration of their Live server penalty.

      9.10.​      Unless expressly stated otherwise, offenses and infringements of these rules are
                  punishable, whether or not they were committed intentionally or successfully.
                  Attempts to commit such offenses or infringements are also punishable.

      9.11.​      Harassment is forbidden. Harassment is defined as systematic, hostile and
                  repeated acts taking place over a considerable period of time, or a singular
                  egregious instance, which is/are intended to isolate or ostracize a person and/or
                  affect the dignity of the person.

      9.12.​      Sexual harassment is forbidden. Sexual harassment is defined as unwelcome
                  sexual advances. The assessment is based on whether a reasonable person
                  would regard the conduct as undesirable or offensive. There is zero tolerance for
                  any sexual threats/coercion or the promise of advantages in exchange for sexual
                  favors.
