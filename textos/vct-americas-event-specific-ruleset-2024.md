1.   Introduction and Purpose

     1.1.       Background.

     VALORANT Champions Tour LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (“League Entity”), along
     with its affiliates (collectively, “Riot”), organizes a series of regional, cross-regional, and
     international esports tournaments and related media events under the name VALORANT
     Champions Tour (the “VCT”) which feature competitive play (both live and online) of the video
     game VALORANT (the “Game”) by professional esports players and teams. The VCT is composed
     of three member leagues: (i) VCT Americas, (ii) VCT Pacific, and (iii) VCT EMEA (each such league,
     a “VCT League” or “League”). The League Entity has entered into written agreements with
     certain team-owning entities (each, a “Team Entity”) governing right to compete in the VCT and
     their respective Leagues (each such agreement, a “Team Participation Agreement”). Pursuant to
     the Team Participation Agreements and as more fully described therein, the League Entity has
     granted each Team Entity the right to field a team that will compete in the VCT and its respective
     League (“Team”).

     1.2.       Purpose.

     The League Entity has created this VCT Americas Event-Specific Ruleset (the “Event-Specific
     Ruleset”) to set forth certain policies, rules, and procedures that will apply to all Teams
     participating in the VCT Americas event, as well as their professional players signed to a Team’s
     official roster (each, a “Player”), owners, Coaches, managers (collectively with Players, “Team
     Members”), and other employees. This Event-Specific Ruleset is in addition to, and not in lieu of,
     the VALORANT Official Competition Ruleset (the “Competition Ruleset”) and the VCT23 Roster
     Construction Rules (the “RCR”).

2.   VCT Americas Event Structure

     2.1.   Kickoff Group Stage Format

     Eleven teams will play in three GSL groups, with teams being seeded based on 2023 VCT
     performance. Each Match will be best-of-three (“Bo3”) Maps. For each Match, Team A and Team
     B in the Map Selection Process (as defined in Section 4.6.8 of the Competition Ruleset) will be
     determined via coin toss, with Team A and B directly determined by the result of the toss. For
     clarity, Teams will not choose to be Team A or B in the Map Selection Process. The two sides of
     the coin will represent the two possibilities of Team A or B in a given Match.


     2.2.   Kickoff Play-Ins Format

     The second-place team from each group at the end of the Regular Season will play in a Single
     Round Robin. Each Match will be best-of-three (“Bo3”) Maps. For each Match, Team A and Team
     B in the Map Selection Process (as defined in Section 4.6.8 of the Competition Ruleset) will be
     determined via coin toss, with Team A and B directly determined by the result of the toss. For
     clarity, Teams will not choose to be Team A or B in the Map Selection Process. The two sides of
     the coin will represent the two possibilities of Team A or B in a given Match. Any ties will be
     broken using the Tiebreaker rules in Section 2.3 of this document.


     2.3.   Kickoff Tiebreakers

             VCT Americas will use the following tiebreaker processes to determine placement of
             Teams within a tie. Tiebreakers will begin from the highest step on each list, progressing
             down each step if the tie is unresolved after the preceding step.

            2.3.1. Tiebreakers Involving Two (2) Teams

                 1.   Head-to-head Match score
                 2.   Head-to-head Map differential
                 3.   Head-to-head Round differential
                 4.   Total Regular Season Map differential
                 5.   Total Regular Season Round differential

            2.3.2. Tiebreakers Involving Three (3) Teams

                 1. Head-to-head Match score for all three Teams
                       a. If a Team has a winning Head-to-head Match score against both of the
                           other Teams in the tie, that Team will claim the highest placement
                           within the tie. The remaining two Teams will contest a new tie according
                           to the tiebreaker process detailed in Section 2.2.1.
                       b. If no Team has a winning Head-to-head Match score against both of the
                           other Teams in the tie (i.e., all Teams hold a 1-1 Head-to-head Match
                           score), proceed to the following step 2.

2.4.   Kickoff Playoffs Format

The winning teams from each Regular Season group, as well as the winner of the Play-In, shall
qualify for Playoffs. Playoffs will be a Single-Elimination bracket, with the Semi-Final matches
being best-of-three series, and the Final being a best-of-five series. Teams shall be seeded in
Playoffs via a random drawing to determine the groups. In the event that the Play-In winner is
selected to play against the winning team from their Regular Season Group, the drawing will be
redone.

       2.4.1. Round 1 - Semi-Finals

               Round 1 will consist of two best-of-three Matches, designated M17 and M18.
               The winner of M17 shall advance to play the winner of M18 in Round 2. Third
               place will be determined among the losers of matches M17 and M18 using the
               following criteria:

                        a.       Total Kickoff Map differential
                        b.       Total Kickoff Round differential

       2.4.2. Round 2 - Final

               Round 2 will consist of one best-of-five match, designated M19. Both the winner
               and runner up will qualify for VCT Masters Madrid, with the winning team
               receiving the #1 regional seed and the runner up receiving the #2 regional seed.
               The Kickoff Playoff winner will also receive 3 Championship Points toward the
               2024 VCT Championship.

       2.4.3. Control of Map Selection Process
               For Semi-Final Playoff matches, control of Map selection will be determined in
               one of two ways. In the match where a Regular Season Group Winner is playing
               against the Play-In Winner, control of Map selection will be granted to the
               Regular Season Group Winner. In the match where two Group Winners are
               against each other, control of Map selection will be determined by coin toss. For
               the Final match, control of Map selection will again be determined by coin toss,
               with Team A and B directly determined by the result of the toss. For clarity,
               Teams will not choose to be Team A or B in the Map Selection Process. The two
               sides of the coin will represent the two possibilities of Team A or B in a given
               Match.

2.6.    Stage 1 Format - Group Stage

Eleven teams will be divided into two groups, Alpha and Omega. Each team from Group Alpha
will play each team in Group Omega once over the course of Stage 1. Each Match will be
best-of-three (“Bo3”) Maps. For each Match, Team A and Team B in the Map Selection Process
(as defined in Section 4.6.8 of the Competition Ruleset) will be determined via coin toss, with
Team A and B directly determined by the result of the toss. For clarity, Teams will not choose to
be Team A or B in the Map Selection Process. The two sides of the coin will represent the two
possibilities of Team A or B in a given Match.

Each Group Stage match win, exclusive of Bye wins, will be worth one (1) Championship Point.

2.8.    Stage 1 Format - Playoffs

The top three (3) teams from each Group, as defined by number of Match wins and subsequent
tiebreakers, will qualify for Playoffs. Teams must win at least two (2) matches in order to qualify
for Playoffs. In the event that less than three (3) teams from a single Group have earned less

than the required number of Match wins, the next highest ranking team from the opposing
group shall be granted a Playoff slot, so long as they meet the qualification requirements. If that
group does not have a fourth team with at least two (2) wins, then the seed will go back to the
original group’s third-highest-ranked team in the standings.

Playoffs will be a Mixed-Elimination bracket, with all matches being best-of-three series, with the
exception of the Lower Finals and Grand Finals, which will each be a best-of-five series.

In the event that three teams from each Group qualify for playoffs, all teams from Group Alpha
will be on one side of the bracket and all teams from Group Omega will be on the other side. The
top seed from each group will receive a Bye into the Upper Semifinals. The second and third seed
teams from Group Alpha will play each other in Round 1, as will the second and third seed teams
from Group Omega.

In the event that four or five teams from a single Group and only one or two teams from the
opposite Group qualify for playoffs, the top seed from each Group will receive a Bye into Upper
Finals. All four remaining teams will be placed into two pools based on record, regardless of
group. The first Pool will be drawn as higher seeds for the Round 1 matches, and Pool 2 will be
randomly drawn as their opponents.

In the event that six teams from a single Group qualify for playoffs, the top two seeds will receive
a Bye into Upper Finals. The second and third seeds will be placed into pools and drawn into
groups randomly so that a second seed plays a third seed in each Round 1 match.

Each Playoff match win, including Bye wins, will be worth one (1) Championship Point, with the
exception of Grand Finals, which will be worth three (3) Championship Points.

2.11.   Stage 2 Tiebreakers

        Teams within a tie. Tiebreakers will begin from the highest step on each list, progressing
        down each step if the tie is unresolved after the preceding step.

        2.11.1. Tiebreakers Involving Two (2) Teams

            1.  Stage 1 Group Stage & Stage 2 Group Stage head-to-head Match record
            2.  Stage 1 Group Stage & Stage 2 Group Stage Map differential
            3.  Stage 1 Group Stage & Stage 2 Group Stage Round differential
            4.  A Bo1 Match is held for a tiebreaker
                    a. The Bo1 Match will be implemented at the discretion of League Officials.

        2.11.2. Tiebreakers Involving Three (3) Teams

            1. Stage 1 Group Stage & Stage 2 Group stage head-to-head Match record for all
               three teams
                   a. The team with a two matchup head-to-head advantage automatically
                       receives first of the group. Tie-breakers for the remaining two teams are

3.   Roster Rules

     3.1.   Starting Rosters

     For each match of the VCT Americas Competition, the Team must designate five (5) starters from
     the Team’s Active Roster (as defined in the RCR), which shall constitute the Team’s “Starting
     Roster”. Rosters are considered public at the submission deadline.

     Requests to modify a Starting Roster for a Team’s first match on any day must be submitted no
     later than 8:00pm local time, or an hour after the conclusion of the final game of the day,
     whichever is the latest, the day prior to the team’s match.

     3.2.   VCT Transfer Window

     The VCT 2024 transfer window closes on July 15, 2024 at 5:00pm in the time zone of a League’s
     Hub City (“VCT Transfer Window”) (see Section 1.2. of VCT24 Roster Construction Rules).

     Once the Transfer Window closes, Teams are prohibited from adding any player to their Full
     Team Roster (as defined in the VCT24 Roster Construction Rules).

     3.3.    Substitutions

     For Matches involving more than one map (i.e., a best-of-three or best-of-five Match), a Team
     may replace their then-current starters with substitutes from the Team’s Active Roster (as
     defined in the VCT24 Roster Construction Rules) in between maps, provided that the Team
     informs the opposing Team and receives approval from the referees of such substitution no later
     than two (2) minutes after the conclusion of the previous game. In the event that a player
     disconnects during a map and is unable to return within the allocated pause time, the Team will
     be permitted to replace the player with a substitute from their Active Roster. Any substitutions
     must result in the Team having an eligible roster.

     Once a Team decides to use a substitution, the substitute player must immediately enter the
     Match Area and will be given seven (7) minutes to complete the setup process.

4.3.      Peripheral Policy

       All Team-Owned Equipment must be submitted to League Officials in advance for approval.
       Approved equipment will remain on-site with League Officials and will only be accessible
       before the Match or at such time as approved by the Head Referee. Unapproved equipment
       or equipment that is suspected by League Officials of providing an unfair competitive
       advantage will not be permitted for use, and Players will be required to use League-Provided
       Equipment instead. At their discretion, League Officials may disallow the use of any
       individual piece of equipment for reasons relating to tournament security, safety or
       operational efficiency or effectiveness. No Player-owned or Team-owned hardware or

       wishes to make changes to their team-owned equipment, notice must be provided to
       League Officials at least one (1) week prior to the team’s next match.

programs MS Paint and Notepad during a Match.

League Officials may prohibit Players and Coaches from accessing native programs in cases of
abuse.

5.   Venue, Competition Area Layout, and Schedule

     5.1.      Match Area

     The “Match Area” is the area immediately surrounding any competition PCs used during Match
     play at a LAN or online event. During Match play, the presence of Team Members in the Match
     Area is restricted solely to the Players of the Teams in play that are participating in the Match,
     and the Match Coaches in accordance with Section 5.1.1.

               5.1.1. Coach Match Area Access

                       The Team may designate a minimum of one (1) and a maximum of two (2)
                       coaches that will participate on stage during the Match (“Match Coaches”), one
                       of which is required to be the Head Coach. The Match Coaches for Teams
                       participating in a given day’s Matches will be granted Match Area access during
                       those Matches, and will be allowed to communicate with their Team during the
                       map pick/ban, agent selection process, and during Timeouts. Match Coaches
                       must be declared at 5pm local time two calendar days before the start of a
                       Competitive Week for that Week. Teams may remove, but not add, Match
                       Coaches during or between Matches in any given Competitive Week. Teams
                       must require valid proof of work eligibility for any elected Match Coach.

6.   Match Process

     6.1.      Player Ready State

     No fewer than two minutes before an official Match is scheduled to begin, a referee will confirm
     with each Player that their setup is complete and will instruct all Players to enter the Match
     lobby. Once all ten Players in a Match have confirmed completion of setup and have entered the
     Match lobby, Players may not enter a warm-up Match or leave the Match Area. Once all ten
     Players in a Match have confirmed completion of setup and have entered the Match lobby, all
     settings will be considered complete and no pauses or delay of the Match will be allowed for
     minor settings issues.

                      Appendix A - VCT Americas Audio and Video COVID Process

If a player meets criteria that prevents them from competing in the Match Area per the COVID policy,
then the following process will occur. This process is to ensure the teams have audio for team
communication and video for both competitive integrity and broadcast purposes.

Step 1: The team manager of the impacted team will pick up the required amount of webcams and/or
Pi's for players and/or coaches who will be playing remotely.

Step 2: The teams are responsible for setting up the video equipment at the remote location where
teams will be playing.

Step 3: The teams will be required to have TeamSpeak and Discord set up. Referees will send the server
information to the teams.

Step 4: Ready check process ensuring all equipment is working correctly.

Webcams and broadcast video
Video will be used for competitive integrity purposes and for broadcast.

Note: Players/coaches who do not test positive or have not been in Close Contact will be required to play
on-stage.

Coaches and analysts will not be allowed to be in the same room as the players. Coaches will be
expected to have a Pi for broadcast. If a coach or analyst does not have a Pi they will be required to have
a webcam.

Coach/analyst will be in Discord with a web cam and referee in the call.

Communication
   ● Coach/analyst will be in Discord with a referee
   ● Players will be in a local TeamSpeak server for voice comms. Coaches will also be in the server
     but the referee who will mute/unmute them during authorized moments of communication. A
     referee will be in the TS channel.

Video
Coaches will have a 5 box vmix sent to them if the coach is remote and players are in the arena. Remote
coaches will be able to call timeouts and participate as they would in the studio.

Last Updated: March 14, 2023

This COVID-19 Control and Prevent Policy (the “ COVID-19 Policy”) has been implemented to
protect the health and safety of participants, tournament staff, and fans at LAN events of the
VCT and the Leagues.

League Entity reserves the right to apply this COVID-19 Policy and make decisions under it
based on its own business judgment, taking into consideration its assessment of the best
interests of League Entity and Riot, and the short and long term interests of the VCT, the
Leagues, and the fans of the game. A decision by League Entity to grant or withhold its consent
or make any other determination under this COVID-19 Policy does not create a precedent that
League Entity is required to follow in any subsequent situation.

This COVID-19 Policy is incorporated into the Competition Ruleset and made applicable to, and
legally binding on, all Owners of a Team that competes in the VCT. Any violation of this Policy is
a violation of the Rules by both the Team and the Owners of the Team.

1. Health and Safety Procedures
Riot will be using the LA County guidelines and recommendations regarding testing and
masking. This may be subject to change. Masking will be dependent on the LA County tier
based system according to LA County’s current COVID Risk.

If you have symptoms, we urge you to take a Lab Based PCR test or a Rapid Antigen Self Test.
If you test negative you will be cleared to enter the Riot Games Arena but we recommend you
still mask regardless of the LA County Risk Tier. If you are a player and do not feel well enough
to play at the Arena, reach out to Player Management.

If you test positive, please reach out to Player Management to inform them of your status. From
there, they will help to determine alternate arrangements for game days.

Isolation, will be required for at least 5 days for those who test positive.. The day you first
experienced symptoms OR the day you tested positive will be your Day 0. On the 6th day, you
may return if you no longer have symptoms AND have tested negative on a Rapid Antigen Self
Test. If you test positive again after the 5 Days, you must isolate for 5 more days.

COVID testing will not be available at Riot Games Arena. Please be mindful of your individual
health and do not attempt to come to the Arena while you are knowingly ill.
