                                             2024 VCT Americas Penalty Index

This Penalty Index is meant to be a guideline for VCT Americas discipline and applicable to most violations. Mitigating factors can reduce any
monetary penalty by a maximum of 25%. The only mitigating factor currently recognized by VCT Americas is self-reporting. Aggravating factors
may increase a monetary penalty by a maximum of 25%. The only aggravating factors currently recognized by the League are non-cooperation
and deception around an infraction (with additional factors recognized in regards to Appendix A and exploit adjudication). The Limitation Period
is the look-back period for previous violations of the same category for the purpose of more severe penalties. For particularly egregious conduct,
VCT Americas reserves the right to review the infraction and enforce penalties in excess of this Penalty Index.

Finally, for competitive infractions that may result in a game-impacting penalty, the opposing Team may be awarded Selection Control. “Selection
Control” is defined as either: (a) having selection of Team A or B during map selection when no predetermined higher seed is available (which
would ordinarily be determined via coin flip), or (b) having both opening map bans in cases where a Team has the privilege of choosing to be
Team A or B during a map selection process. League Officials may award Selection Control in one of these fashions in order to be appropriately
punitive for any given competitive infraction.

                                                  Appendix A - Exploit Adjudication

    1. Purpose: Appendix A exists to delineate penalties for usage of in-game exploits listed on the Bug and Exploit list and usage
       of exploits that League Officials have given specific notice to Players to avoid, including exploits listed in any applicable VCT
       Rule Set. A one-size-fits-all solution does not function in VALORANT, as exploits may have varying levels of impact on
       Rounds and Matches. This appendix will detail each level of exploit and the penalties associated with usage of exploits of
       each level.

    2. Exploit Levels: There are five levels of exploit, distinguished by severity, previous notice, intention, and timing of discovery.
       Each base level is characterized as follows:

             a. Level 1: An exploit that had no or negligible impact on the outcome of a Round.
             b. Level 2: An exploit that had a significant impact on the outcome of a Round, but the intention of the Player can not be
                determined.
             c. Level 3: An exploit that had a significant impact on the outcome of a Round, and League Officials have determined
                that the Player has executed this exploit intentionally.
             d. Level 4: An exploit that had a significant impact on the outcome of a Game, but the Game has concluded and round
                rollback and/or remake is not possible.
             e. Level 5: An exploit that had a significant impact on the outcome of a Game, but the Match has concluded and no
                reasonable mitigation steps are available.

        League Officials will, at their sole discretion, determine the severity of any used exploit and the intention of the Player having
        used the exploit. In cases of repeat offense, League Officials may also escalate the level of the exploit in question. For
        instance, if a Player uses the same exploit again, after League Officials issued the Player a penalty for a previous usage of
        that exploit, League Officials may then treat the exploit as Level 2 due to the repeated offense. League Officials may also
        escalate the level of an exploit in egregious cases with regards to intention, malice, or excessive usage.
